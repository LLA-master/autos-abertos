Who's who

Short bios of the people, companies and institutions a reader needs to hold in mind, written for an audience outside Brazil. Every fact below comes from the public case file as summarised in this site's chronicles, and each card says which filing states it. Allegations are attributed to whoever made them: the Federal Police (PF), the Prosecutor General's Office (PGR), the Central Bank, the reporting justice, or the defence. Nobody described here has been charged, let alone convicted; all of it is investigation-stage material assessed by a judge on a summary basis. The bank and the family

Where the money was raised, and who the police say controlled it.

Banco Master

Private bank, São Paulo · formerly Banco Máxima · liquidated 18 November 2025

A mid-sized bank that, according to the Federal Police summary the reporting justice endorsed on a summary basis, funded itself by selling bank deposit certificates (CDBs) at yields far above the market and poured the money into high-risk operations, illiquid assets and investment funds linked to its own group. The police hypothesis in the Brasília inquiry puts the certificates issued at roughly R$ 50 billion, with about R$ 12 billion possibly uncovered.

On 18 November 2025 the Central Bank ordered the extrajudicial liquidation of Banco Master and three group companies, appointing a liquidator. The police later estimated the hole the deposit-guarantee fund was covering at close to R$ 40 billion. The ruling of 3 March 2026 describes four alleged cells around the bank: financial, institutional corruption, concealment and laundering, and intimidation.

Sources: PET 15556 seq 16 pp. 1–2, 23–26; INQ 5026 seq 10 pp. 3750–3751; INQ 5026 seq 12 pp. 10–30. Chronicles: "Seventy-two hours", "Twelve billion and two associations", "The eighteenth of November".

Daniel Bueno Vorcaro

Controlling shareholder of Banco Master · in pre-trial detention since 3 March 2026

The banker at the centre of the case and the most connected person in this archive: 141 narrative filings across all fifteen proceedings. The record describes him as the bank's controller. A Brasília federal judge first ordered his pre-trial detention on 17 November 2025, in the inquiry about the sale of loan portfolios to the state-owned BRB; he was arrested the next morning as, his defence said, he was about to fly to Dubai to sign the sale of the bank to a consortium. The federal appellate court TRF1 released him on 28 November 2025 under restrictions, including an ankle monitor. In Pet 15.562 the police describe, from the same phone, an "armed wing" paid through his brother-in-law's companies, notes listing the police officers at a closed meeting and the judge's name before the first operation, and a whiteboard headed "TITAN CAYMAN" found in his office; the third-phase searches of 4 March 2026 covered two of his homes.

On 3 March 2026 Justice André Mendonça ordered his pre-trial detention again, this time on the police allegations concerning the surveillance cell known as "A Turma" and payments to Central Bank supervisors; the ruling transcribes messages attributed to him and states there are "strong indications" that he ordered a staged robbery against a journalist. The Second Panel unanimously confirmed the order. He was moved to the federal penitentiary in Brasília on 5 March 2026, invoked his right to silence before the congressional inquiry committee, and in July 2026 the securities regulator was authorised to question him in prison. No indictment appears in the published record.

Sources: INQ 5026 seq 10 pp. 3763–3765 and seq 12 pp. 513–535; PET 15556 seq 16 pp. 5–14, 23–26, 40–42, seq 84, seq 294, seq 478; INQ 5026 seq 453. Chronicles: the Pet 15.556 and INQ 5.026 series. PET 15562 seq 1 pp. 3–9, 129–149, seq 54. Chronicles: the Pet 15.562 series.

Henrique Moura Vorcaro

Daniel Vorcaro's father · account of R$ 2.245 billion frozen · detained under another petition

In the second phase of the operation, on 14 January 2026, the police froze R$ 2,245,235,850.24 in his account at a securities dealer known as REAG; the police representation frames this as money hidden from creditors while the deposit-guarantee fund "bled". His defence answered that the amount is tied up in a private-equity fund backed by carbon credits and has no link to Banco Master; the justice kept the freeze without ruling on the merits. At his home in Belo Horizonte the police seized an Aston Martin, a Bentley, a Range Rover and other cars, watches and iPads; his lawyers, who called him a "peripheral figure" included for family ties, asked for the cars back and were refused in July 2026.

A procedural order of 6 July 2026 reveals that by then he was in pre-trial detention under a separate petition, Pet 15.978, in connection with "A Turma", an order confirmed by the Second Panel. That petition is not yet covered by the chronicles.

Sources: PET 15556 seq 16 pp. 23–26, seq 440; PET 15198 seq 21, seq 314, seq 1004, seq 1012 pp. 5–6. Chronicles: "The small hours of 14 January", "The cars", "After the order".

Felipe Cançado Vorcaro and Natália Vorcaro

Daniel Vorcaro's brother and sister · search targets on 14 January 2026

Both were among the 39 targets of the January 2026 searches. The police link them, with their father, to two holding companies through two real-estate firms. Felipe asked in March 2026 for the release of 21 properties, two vehicles and bank balances, arguing he never took part in the bank's management or ownership and that the two firms never operated and were dissolved in 2022; the request was denied in May 2026. Fabiano Campos Zettel

Described by the police as the group's "financial operator" and Vorcaro's brother-in-law · in pre-trial detention since 3 March 2026

According to the 3 March 2026 ruling, he handled the payments to the surveillance cell and, through the same structure, the payments to two Central Bank officials; the money left the company Super Participações. The ruling quotes messages such as "today we have to pay Belline's first one". On the night of 13 January 2026 the police told Justice Toffoli he held a ticket to Dubai for 00:50; the justice ordered a temporary detention until seven in the morning so he could not warn relatives before the searches, and the seizure of his passport. Five days after receiving a safe-conduct to appear before the congressional inquiry, he was detained under the March ruling. Requests for a private room for lawyer visits and a television in his cell were denied in July 2026. In Pet 15.562 the police transcribe his "Every month I send it" about the R$ 1 million to Mourão's company and his "Sicário has the names and where to find them?" about the boat captain; at the 4 March 2026 search of his apartment, entered through a broken service door, the police seized a Rolex, a R$ 190,000 necklace and three sheets with screenshots of a Federal Police intelligence system.

Employee of Vorcaro and partner in Super Empreendimentos · alternative measures with ankle monitor

"A Turma": the alleged surveillance cell

Not to be confused with the Second Panel (Segunda Turma) of the Supreme Court. "A Turma", "the crew", is what the police say the group called itself.

Luiz Phillipi Machado de Moraes Mourão

"Felipe Mourão" in the messages · alleged coordinator of "A Turma" · died 6 March 2026

The ruling describes him as the coordinator of the fourth cell, providing Vorcaro with "sensitive information, monitoring of people and neutralisation of situations considered sensitive", allegedly through improper access to restricted government systems, including, the police say, those of the Federal Police, the federal prosecutors and international bodies. The ruling finds "strong indications" that he received one million reais a month, and transcribes his message about dividing "the monthly" among "the crew". He was detained on 3 March 2026; a firearm found at his arrest led to a separate charge. He died three days later; the circumstances are not in the record. On 29 May 2026 the justice declared the State's power to punish him extinguished, keeping the asset freezes for his heirs and his messages as evidence against others. In Pet 15.562 the police say his crew, "A Turma", looked up sealed cases in the prosecutors' and police systems, sent a forged Interpol document and a forged prosecutor's request to Meta to take down profiles, and was sent after a former boat captain and a chef; they put his receipts since October 2023 at more than R$ 24 million.

Sources: PET 15556 seq 16 pp. 9–15, seq 58, seq 441 pp. 2–5. Chronicles: "The crew", "After the order". PET 15562 seq 1 pp. 60–120, seq 3 pp. 190–198. Chronicles: "A Turma, the crew", "Five conversations".

Marilson Roseno da Silva

Retired Federal Police clerk · in pre-trial detention since 3 March 2026

According to the record he was one of the main operators of the coercion cell, using the experience and contacts of his police career to obtain sensitive data and watch targets, under Mourão's operational lead, in order to "anticipate or neutralise risks" from official investigations, journalists and former employees. In June 2026 the justice refused to unfreeze his accounts but allowed his pension, found to be unrelated to the alleged crimes, to be paid into his wife's account to support her and a minor daughter. The wife and daughter are not named in the record or here. In Pet 15.562 the police say they identified him through a condominium visitor log in Nova Lima on 6 April 2024, and that "A Turma" received R$ 400,000 a month to split among six people, of whom he is the only one identified.

Inside the regulator

Two officials of the department that supervised the bank, and the consultancy through which the police say they were paid.

Belline Santana

Head of the Central Bank's bank-supervision department (Desup) · alternative measures, suspended from office

The ruling states that he provided the bank's controller with strategic advice on its regulatory situation, reviewed drafts of letters the bank would send to the very department he headed, held private meetings with Vorcaro outside the Central Bank's premises and sat in a message group with him and his deputy. According to the police, he received a simulated consultancy contract, through a firm called Varajo, whose stated object was a study on "bringing young people into the financial market" and whose accounts served as a pass-through. The Central Bank had already removed him from his post. No detention was requested; he was barred from entering the Central Bank, had his passport withdrawn and wears an ankle monitor. The evidence was shared with the Central Bank and, in July 2026, with the federal comptroller for a disciplinary and asset inquiry. In Pet 15.504 he is formally under investigation in the police inquiry opened in May 2026; questioned on 25 August 2026, he said he negotiated the contract only with Palhares, never met Zettel, received R$ 3.815 million in total and consulted the Central Bank's internal-affairs office (Corregedoria) informally, without mentioning the amount or Vorcaro's name. In Pet 15.562 two phones and two laptops were seized at his flat on 4 March 2026, with the passwords handed over; on 22 July 2026 the justice allowed a certified copy, provided the defence supplies factory-sealed hard drives with the purchase receipt.

Sources: PET 15556 seq 16 pp. 6, 17–21, 32–34, 37–38, 42–44, seq 258, seq 480; INQ 5026 seq 12 pp. 528–535; PET 15504 seq 44 pp. 1–2, seq 92 pp. 85–110. Chronicles: "The supervisor and the letter", "Getting to the other side". PET 15562 seq 105, seq 127, seq 133. Chronicle: "The sealed boxes".

Paulo Sérgio Neves de Souza

Deputy head of Desup at the time · alternative measures, suspended from office

The ruling describes "direct and frequent" contact with Vorcaro: he sent him the image of his own appointment order, suggested how to behave in a meeting with the Central Bank's president, edited a letter the bank was sending to his own department and warned the bank in advance of transactions flagged by the regulator's monitoring systems. The justice writes that he became "a kind of employee or consultant of Vorcaro for purely private matters". A trip to Orlando for which Vorcaro said he would arrange a guide is listed as a further indication of corruption. He received the same measures as his superior. The congressional inquiry later reported, citing the press, that he was complying with them at a residence in Guaxupé, Minas Gerais. In Pet 15.504 the police add the sale of a farm to a company of Vorcaro's brother-in-law (R$ 4.8 million in the side agreement, R$ 3 million in the deed) and a house sold to another of his companies; questioned on 10 July 2026, he said the messages are ordinary supervision, that his director told him to "hold off" (jogar parado) in September 2024 and that his team drafted 23 liquidation votes. In Pet 15.562 the police note that the Central Bank document the TRF-1 relied on to free Vorcaro, about the 17 November 2025 video call, is signed by him and records that the call was not recorded and no minutes were taken; his own requests for the return of his laptops, one of them needed to answer a tax-authority summons, were declared duplicates of Pet 15.556 on 13 July 2026.

Leonardo Augusto Furtado Palhares

Lawyer and company administrator · administrator of Varajo · alternative measures

"Léo" in the messages. The ruling attributes to him the signing and formalisation of the simulated consultancy proposal, "lending the contract an appearance of legitimacy", and quotes Vorcaro's "see whether Léo can pay, and we reimburse the next day". He received the ankle-monitor package without the Central Bank clause, since he is not a civil servant. Before the congressional inquiry he invoked, besides silence, professional privilege as a lawyer. To the Federal Police, on 15 June 2026, he said the project was requested by Zettel, that the R$ 2 million figure "was suggested" to him, that the money came from Moriah Asset and later from Super, and that the project nonetheless existed. In Pet 15.562 his home was searched on 4 March 2026; as a lawyer he asked for the Bar's safeguards and a mirror copy of his devices, and on 30 March complained that the two dockets had been made available to him "mixed", without page numbers or dates.

Super Empreendimentos, Moriah Asset, Varajo and the two King companies

Five companies suspended by court order on 3 March 2026

The ruling suspended five companies it describes as run by members of the alleged structure and used for fictitious consultancy contracts, group-linked investments, intermediation, simulated asset deals and the payment of illicit expenses, finding "no element pointing to the actual performance of lawful economic activity". Super Empreendimentos e Participações is described as the box from which the transfers to "A Turma" and to the officials left; in the São Paulo petition, a company of the same name receives R$ 650 million at the end of a chain the Central Bank traced through REAG funds. The chronicle notes the record links the two halves of the case through that name without the site asserting they are one. King Participações Imobiliárias is described by the police as Mourão's company.

The Brasília deal: where the operation began

Inquiry 5.026 concerns the sale of loan portfolios by Banco Master to a state-owned bank. This is the first phase of Operation Compliance Zero, November 2025.

BRB, Banco de Brasília

State-owned bank controlled by the government of the Federal District · the buyer

According to the Central Bank, R$ 12.2 billion flowed from BRB to Banco Master in the first months of 2025: R$ 6.7 billion for loan portfolios and R$ 5.5 billion as a premium; the police count R$ 16.7 billion between July 2024 and October 2025, the source of the "R$ 17 billion" later cited in Congress. The portfolios equalled 30% of BRB's assets. The Central Bank found that BRB assessed the portfolio only after buying it, breached its Basel capital ratio in early 2025 and booked entries without supporting documents; the police say the bank "always wanted to lend money to Banco Master" and could not do so directly because of exposure limits. When the Central Bank asked about the loans' origin, Master first named two civil-servant associations in Bahia and then a company called Tirreno; a Central Bank test of a sample found no matching loans. BRB's president and finance director were removed for 60 days by the November 2025 ruling, which found indications of fraudulent management but a "considerably lower" danger; neither was detained.

Sources: INQ 5026 seq 10 pp. 3748–3772. Chronicles: "Twelve billion and two associations", "BRB's side", "The eighteenth of November".

Tirreno and Cartos

A shelf company renamed two days before the contract, and the direct-credit company behind it

Tirreno was created in November 2024 under another name, renamed and recapitalised from R$ 100 to R$ 30 million on 2 December 2024, and signed a partnership contract with Banco Master three days later. The Central Bank found that its only relationship within the financial system was with Master and that it had never made a single payment. The prosecutors speak of "serial forgery of documents"; the judge of "possible document fraud". Its capital was paid in by Henrique Peretto, a partner in Cartos, a licensed direct-credit company that the police say originated the sampled loans and with which Master allegedly "unlawfully associated" to produce and resell non-existent portfolios. Tirreno's director, a former Master employee, and Peretto were held for three days in November 2025.

Sources: INQ 5026 seq 10 pp. 3750–3765. Chronicles: "Twelve billion and two associations", "The eighteenth of November".

Tirreno Cartos

Augusto Ferreira Lima, Luiz Antonio Bull and the other Master directors

Detained 18 November 2025 · released by TRF1 on 28 November 2025 under restrictions

The Brasília judge described Augusto Lima as a partner and director of Banco Master who indirectly controlled the two Bahia associations named as the origin of the loans; Luiz Antonio Bull and Ângelo Ribeiro da Silva as the directors who signed the Tirreno contracts and the letter to the Central Bank; and Alberto Felix as a director who signed the partnership contract. All four were released ten days later. Their defences say, respectively, that Lima sold his shares to Vorcaro in May 2024 and never controlled the associations, one of which predates his birth; that Bull, 69, returned home voluntarily to present himself; and that Felix was never a statutory director and signed only to make up a quorum. The chronicle's verdict on the dispute: "who is right, this text does not know."

Sources: INQ 5026 seq 10 pp. 3763–3765, seq 12 pp. 10–30, 513–538, seq 273. Chronicle: "The eighteenth of November".

The São Paulo funds

Petition 15.198 is the second phase, January 2026: an inquiry born in São Paulo that the Supreme Court pulled up to Brasília.

REAG and João Carlos Falbo Mansur

Securities dealer group · Mansur described by the Central Bank as its controller · search target

The Central Bank traced a chain in which Banco Master disbursed R$ 1.45 billion in 2024 and got R$ 1.38 billion back through certificates bought by two REAG funds; a redemption of about R$ 800 million then moved R$ 650 million through a private-equity fund to Super Empreendimentos. A third fund passed from the funds to Vorcaro personally at the end of 2024. The Central Bank reported "doubts as to the real controller". Mansur was added as a target in November 2025 on a Central Bank communication to prosecutors; the declared beneficiaries of the funds are three relatives who are not named here. Henrique Moura Vorcaro's frozen R$ 2.245 billion sat in an account at the group's dealer.

Sources: PET 15198 seq 14 pp. 12–14, seq 21, seq 512. Chronicles: "From São Paulo to Brasília", "The funds and the notes".

Reag Trust Mansur

The single-quotaholder funds and the clinic

Where the police say the deposit money went

The prosecutors and police allege that Banco Master directed a large part of the money raised through deposit certificates into receivables funds of which the bank itself was the sole quotaholder, and that of more than R$ 3.5 billion so invested about R$ 1.8 billion bought notes issued by companies tied to the bank's own partners. The emblematic example: Clínica Mais Médicos, a company with zero capital and R$ 54,079.64 in 2023 revenue, issued R$ 361 million in unsecured notes; its president, whom the prosecutors call a front and whom this site does not name, had received emergency welfare payments in 2020 and 2021. Another fund's receivables came from a payment company holding 149.98% of the fund's net worth. The requested freeze totalled R$ 5,775,234,097.25.

Sources: PET 15198 seq 14 pp. 7–9, 14–18. Chronicle: "The funds and the notes".

The court, the prosecutors, the police and the regulators

Justice André Mendonça

Reporting justice of all fifteen proceedings since 13 February 2026

The most cited name in the archive, 252 narrative filings, because the reporting justice signs everything. He received the case by redistribution in February 2026; the record does not state why. His 48-page ruling of 3 March 2026 ordered four pre-trial detentions, four sets of alternative measures and the suspension of five companies, after refusing the Prosecutor General's request for more time with the words "time flies". The same ruling devotes seven pages to how the arrests should be carried out: discreetly, without handcuffs beyond what a binding precedent allows, with a custody hearing within 24 hours and any release reserved to himself. He also wrote the standing formulas of this record: safe-conducts for everyone summoned by the congressional inquiry, and refusals of every request to return seized cars. On 10 September 2026 he lifted the seal at the request of the Court's presidency.

Sources: PET 15556 seq 16 (passim), seq 294; PET 15198 seq 403, seq 703, seq 1012. Chronicles: "Seventy-two hours", "What the justice decided, and how", "The new reporting justice and the hundred devices".

Justice Dias Toffoli

Reporting justice until 13 February 2026

Reporting justice of the constitutional complaint that brought the Brasília inquiry to the Supreme Court in December 2025, and of the São Paulo petition until February 2026. He granted the bank-secrecy lifts and asset freezes on 6 January 2026, the searches against 39 targets on 8 January, and on the night of 13 January, told of the flight to Dubai of one of the persons under investigation, ordered a temporary detention until dawn and rebuked the Federal Police in capital letters for "exclusive inertia" and "express and deliberate disregard" of his order of the previous day. His last act in the Brasília inquiry ordered the full content of seized devices sent to the Court. A four-page order by the Court's president, three pages of them blank, redistributed the case. Two people bearing the Toffoli surname received safe-conducts from his successor for the congressional inquiry; the record does not explain why they were summoned, and this site does not speculate.

The Second Panel

Five justices · confirmed the 3 March 2026 detentions unanimously

Under the Court's rules a reporting justice's precautionary orders in criminal matters must be put to a panel. The Second Panel confirmed the March ruling unanimously in a virtual session held between 13 and 20 March 2026; on 31 March the defence's internal appeals were declared moot. The appellate path inside the Supreme Court closed in seventeen days. The same panel confirmed Henrique Moura Vorcaro's detention ordered under Pet 15.978.

Sources: PET 15556 seq 294 p. 3; PET 15198 seq 1004. Chronicles: "What the justice decided, and how", "The new reporting justice and the hundred devices".

The Prosecutor General's Office (PGR)

Paulo Gonet, Prosecutor General · the only body that can bring charges before the Court

The Prosecutor General opened Petition 15.198 in December 2025 with a one-page request under the highest secrecy grade. In January 2026 his office withdrew the São Paulo police's request to detain Vorcaro, noting he was already under restrictions. In March 2026, given 72 hours to opine on the 164-page police request, it let the deadline lapse and then argued that a deadline "counted in hours" was impossible to meet for measures of such gravity, that there was no imminent danger, and that it could not endorse the requests. The justice ruled anyway. The chronicle's summary: "the body that accuses asks for calm; the judge who decides says calm is a risk." No indictment appears in the published record.

Sources: PET 15198 seq 1, seq 14 p. 4; PET 15556 seq 16 pp. 2–3, 30. Chronicles: "From São Paulo to Brasília", "Seventy-two hours".

The Federal Police (PF)

Investigating body · author of Operation Compliance Zero

The police wrote the representations that every ruling in this record transcribes: the November 2025 request in Brasília, the São Paulo request that led to the January 2026 searches, and the 164-page request of 27 February 2026 listing seven crime headings, from criminal organisation and money laundering to corruption and obstruction. The Court's relationship with the police was not always smooth: Justice Toffoli's rebuke of January 2026, and Justice Mendonça's February rules confining the case to the assigned officers and sending the forensic examination of about a hundred seized devices back to the police's ordinary flow.

Sources: PET 15556 seq 1, seq 16; PET 15198 seq 85, seq 403. Chronicles: "Seventy-two hours", "The small hours of 14 January", "The new reporting justice and the hundred devices".

The Central Bank of Brazil

Supervisor of the banks · liquidated the Master group on 18 November 2025

The regulator whose March 2025 letter to Banco Master, asking about the loans sold to BRB, started the Brasília inquiry, and whose reports on BRB's prudential breaches and on the REAG chain are transcribed throughout the record. On the afternoon of 17 November 2025 its supervisors held a video call with Vorcaro in which, according to a Central Bank document, he said he would fly to Dubai that day to sign the bank's sale; the next day it decreed the liquidation. Two of the officials on that call are among the persons under investigation. Its two most recent presidents appear only because Congress summoned them: Justice Mendonça found "no indication whatsoever" of the former president's involvement, the only such statement about anyone in the whole series of rulings, and converted the summons into an invitation.

Sources: INQ 5026 seq 10 pp. 3750–3762, seq 12 pp. 528–535, seq 423; PET 15198 seq 14 pp. 12–14. Chronicles: the INQ 5.026 series, "The supervisor and the letter".

CVM, CGU, FGC and the congressional inquiry

The securities regulator, the federal comptroller, the deposit-guarantee fund, and Congress

The securities regulator (CVM) had already sanctioned Banco Master on at least five earlier occasions, filed a criminal complaint in June 2025 and, in July 2026, was authorised to question Vorcaro in prison in three administrative investigations. The federal comptroller (CGU) obtained the evidence against the two Central Bank officials for a disciplinary and asset inquiry. The deposit-guarantee fund (FGC) is the entity the police say was "bleeding" to cover the bank's hole. The joint congressional inquiry into organised crime summoned most of the persons under investigation, the governor, the former Central Bank president and the two Toffolis; the Court answered each summons with a safe-conduct, and several of them, Vorcaro included, invoked their right to silence.
