Prime You, the floor above.

A company in Alphaville sells fractions of jets, helicopters, yachts and islands. Each asset lives inside a company that is born with a hundred reais and a number, and that changes its name when the asset arrives. This dossier shows the machine, the names it has used, what it keeps in Angra dos Reis and where it touches the Banco Master case. Every fact points to a document.

What this dossier is, and what it is not. It is a reading of public documents: company files from the São Paulo commercial registry (JUCESP), one land-registry certificate from Angra dos Reis, the Brazilian Aircraft Registry, fund portfolios reported to the securities regulator, and the 189,000 pages the Supreme Court made public. It is not a verdict and not an accusation. Shared ownership is a lawful business, and one company per asset is standard practice in business aviation. What is described here are structures that, in a due-diligence reading, call for an explanation — and the company's own explanation, given in the court record, is in chapter 10. Every statement carries a label: proven when it comes from a document read in full, inference when it is a cross-reference, claim when it is what somebody said.

In one sentence: Prime You is the ownership layer for the luxury assets of people who would rather not appear as their owners, and one of that layer's clients was the controlling shareholder of Banco Master. The sentence is harsh, but the first half is the company's own. Introducing itself to the Federal Police, it says it "leads the market as the most substantial and diversified asset-sharing operation in South America", with "more than 45 special-purpose companies incorporated and managed". The second half is also in the record: Viking Participações, Daniel Vorcaro's personal holding company, is "a quotaholder of PS-MAS Administração de Bens Próprios S.A., one of the more than forty S.P.E.s named as clients of Prime You, and the owner of a Helicopter". That was written by Prime You's own lawyer, in November 2025, days after the first phase of Operation Compliance Zero.

The rest of this dossier is what the public registries add to those two sentences. And it is a good deal.

Chapter 1The machine, in four movements

Every company in the group is born the same way, and the commercial registry keeps each one's birth certificate. The name is FRACTION followed by a three-digit number and the words "Administração de Bem Próprio S.A." — roughly, "management of its own asset". The capital is one hundred or one hundred and one reais. The company is closely held, which in plain language means: it does not publish who its shareholders are. It has no asset, no value and no name that links it to anyone. It is a numbered shell.

When the asset arrives, a shareholders' meeting is held. A single set of minutes does three things: the money already sitting inside the company as an "advance for a future capital increase", the AFAC of the local jargon, becomes capital; the capital jumps from a hundred and one reais to millions or hundreds of millions; and the company changes its name to the name of the asset. If it is an aircraft, the new name is the registration painted on the fuselage: PT-XGS, PS-MGG, PP-NYM. If it is a boat, the boat's name: Solar, Sapphire 62. If it is a house, the town: Real Estate Duo 002 Angra dos Reis. This was read in 24 sets of minutes, each with a date, and the oldest is from 2018.

The consequence is not a side effect of the model; it is the model. At the end of the cycle the jet is registered to a company whose name points to no one, whose capital reveals no origin, because it came from an advance whose author is not named, and whose owners appear in no public database at all. To name the first shareholder of one of the group's SPEs, we had to order from the registry the full text of a share subscription form from 2014.

Reading rule number one for this dossier: when a public document says "owner: Fraction 053", it is telling the truth and telling you nothing. The name is the floor below. The owner lives on the floor above.

Chapter 2The island in Angra, or the registry left behind

Start with the document that opened this reading: the certificate of land-registry entry 10.707-A, First Registry Office of Angra dos Reis, issued on 27 January 2026. The property is "geometric projection no. 5-B of the Ilha do Jorge condominium", in the municipality's 2nd District: 23.5 metres of sea frontage, 1,266 m² for exclusive use plus 1,018 m² of common area, an undivided share of 0.6535% of an island of 76,000 square metres. It sits on federal foreshore land, with an occupancy registration at the Federal Property Office and a transfer fee payable to the Union on every sale. It is a beach house on a private island.

Record R.09 is the one that matters. On 9 August 2022, PrimeAviation 2 Participações S.A., a closely held corporation based in Alphaville, bought projection 5-B for R$ 2.5 million, "paid in full". The same entry holds a detail that a hurried reading misses: the earlier deed, from 2016, by which the seller had acquired the same property, stated R$ 50,000, and was only taken to the registry in May 2022, three months before the resale at fifty times that price. There is nothing unlawful about selling dear what you bought cheap, and the transfer fee and the property tax were paid on the full amount. But it is the kind of jump a reader of land registries writes down.

The minutes that authorised the purchase are public too. On 29 July 2022, at ten in the morning, the shareholders' meeting of PrimeAviation 2 approved the acquisition "unanimously and without reservations". Chair: Marcus Vinicius da Mata; secretary: Artur Martins de Figueiredo. Shareholder present, and the only one: Entre Investimentos e Participações Ltda. Remember that name. It comes back in chapter 4 and in chapter 7.

Six months later, on 16 January 2023, the same company, the same chair and secretary and the same sole shareholder approved a far larger purchase: the occupancy rights and improvements of Ilha Cunhambebe Mirim, also in Ribeira Bay, 26,700 m², for R$ 45 million in three instalments. An entire, small island, bought by a company whose name was a number.

And here the machine of chapter 1 shows its most concrete side effect. On 22 September 2025, PrimeAviation 2 changed its name to Domus Realty Participações S.A. The commercial registry recorded it. The Angra land registry was not told, or had not been told by January 2026: the certificate still lists the owner as "PrimeAviation 2". Anyone searching the land registry for "Domus Realty" will not find the house. Anyone searching for "PrimeAviation 2" finds the house of a company that, under that name, no longer exists. For a creditor, for a judge trying to freeze the asset, for a parliamentary inquiry, that is an extra week of work, and sometimes it is the difference between finding it and not.

There is more Angra in the group. Three other companies carry the town in their names: Real Estate Duo 002 Angra dos Reis (formerly Fraction 008, renamed in April 2020), Real Estate Duo 004 Angra dos Reis (formerly Fraction 014, January 2021) and Real Estate Solar Island 005 Angra dos Reis (formerly Fraction 035, April 2025). What properties these are, the commercial registry does not say; the land registry will, when somebody orders the entries. And there is a witness statement, in the Supreme Court record, that speaks of a "house no. 4 on Ilha do Jorge in Angra dos Reis" attributed to Vorcaro. Projection 5-B has "plot 04" on its left boundary. They are neighbouring units, not the same one; the statement is a witness's, unconfirmed by any document in the record. Chapter 3The tree of names

Thirty-two company files were read at the commercial registry. Each file gives the name at incorporation on its first page and, further down, the formula that gives the change away: "change of corporate name to X, pursuant to the extraordinary general meeting held on". Put together, they build the tree below: one trunk, Prime You, and four branches, one per class of asset.

Chapter 4Who owns it: the ownership chain, at last with names

A closely held company does not publish its shareholders, but it does publish the minutes of a capital increase, and those minutes say who subscribed. That was the way in. Nine full-text documents, ordered from the registry, answer the question no public database answers.

Read the left-hand column from the bottom up. The G700, a jet worth 500 million reais, belongs to PS-MGG. PS-MGG had its capital subscribed by PrimeAviation 2, today Domus Realty, the same owner of the house on Ilha do Jorge. And Domus had its capital subscribed by Entre Investimentos, represented by Antonio Carlos Freixo Junior, the "shareholder present" in both sets of Angra minutes. The property layer and the aviation layer are not parallel: one is a shareholder of the other. And Entre Investimentos, as chapter 7 shows, is on the list of one hundred names whose bank secrecy the Supreme Court ordered lifted.

In the middle column, the holding company. The court record states, in Prime You's own words, that "Prime Aviation's partners are Marcus Matta and the Patrimonial Blue fund". Patrimonial Blue is a private-equity fund administered by Trustee DTVM, and the monthly portfolios it reports to the securities regulator show common shares of Prime Aviation Participações from June 2019 onwards. In other words: the president's partner is a fund, and the fund is administered by the institution that reappears in every chapter that follows.

In the right-hand column, the only SPE quotaholder Prime You named of its own accord: Vorcaro's Viking, in PS-MAS. With a detail the aircraft registry adds and the court filing does not: the helicopter PS-MAS operates has a foreign legal entity, SFG Aircraft, Inc., as its registered owner. The Brazilian SPE appears only as the operator. Economic ownership and registered ownership, here, do not coincide. inference as to what that means; proven as to what each registry says.

Chapter 5The circuit: the bank finances the group, the group capitalises the bank

This is the chapter where the dossier stops being about a jet company and starts being about the Banco Master case. Two documents, five years apart, form the two ends of a single circuit.

The 2019 end is modest and, for that very reason, eloquent. On 23 September that year, the shareholders' meeting of what was then Fraction 011 approved "the contracting of a revolving credit facility of up to R$ 2,850,000.00 with Banco Máxima S/A", and in the same minutes renamed the company PT-XGS, after the Learjet it was taking over. Three weeks later, on 14 October, the Central Bank authorised the transfer of control of Máxima to Daniel Vorcaro. Máxima became Master. It is the oldest documented financial relationship between the group and the bank, and it predates by six years everything the press has covered. Whether the credit was drawn, and against what security, the registry does not say. proven as to the dates; the rest is open.

The 2024 end is large. On 5 April, Banvox Holding Financeira issued 400 debentures of one million reais each, with Trustee DTVM as trustee and joint and several guarantees from Daniel Vorcaro, Maurício Antônio Quadrado and Augusto Ferreira Lima. Clause 3.2.1 of the deed, as the court decision transcribes it, says the proceeds "were allocated to the capitalisation of the financial institution Banco Master S.A., of which the individual Defendants are directors". The March and April 2025 instalments went unpaid. Then came acceleration, an enforcement action for R$ 470.5 million, the seizure of ten properties and the attachment of shareholdings. Among them: 6,674,100 of Quadrado's shares in Real Estate Duo 001 Guarujá, a Prime You SPE. It was the first time a shareholder of one of the group's SPEs appeared named in a public document.

The design is this: Banvox, run by Figueiredo and Quadrado, the same men who run Trustee Holding, raises R$ 400 million to put capital into Master; Trustee, which administers the funds that carry the SPEs, is the trustee of the issue; the guarantors are the bank's controlling shareholder and two names who are inside the SPEs; and when the deal breaks, the stock of luxury assets is what answers for it. A year later, in March 2026, Master S/A Corretora, a company of the bank's own group, obtained an injunction recording a notice against disposal over PS-MGG, Domus Realty and Botanique. The bank in liquidation is now litigating against the wealth it helped build.

Issuing debentures to capitalise a bank is lawful. Defaulting is a civil problem, not a criminal one. What the April 2025 decision proves is not a crime: it is that the two groups are a single circuit, and that the circuit was documented in court. The statement that the three guarantors were directors of Master comes from the claimant's pleading, recited by the court; it is not a judicial finding.

There are smaller vectors, all checkable: the PT-MCP helicopter was bought in 2013 with a loan from BTG Pactual "with funds from the BNDES FINAME programme", of up to R$ 5.65 million — money from a public capital-goods programme in a shared-ownership asset; PS-FMV, with R$ 6.87 million of capital, approved in December 2024 a loan of up to R$ 90.2 million, indexed to the exchange rate, from Bradesco Global Private Bank; Sapphire 62 records a bank facility "to finance the acquisition of an Azimut 62 vessel". All of it in the minutes.

Chapter 6The funds: a circuit that administers, manages and owns itself

Ten are administered by Trustee DTVM; one by Master S/A CTVM. The managers repeat: Blue Solutions and MAM Asset.

Chapter 7What the Supreme Court record says, with page numbers

The Court's public record runs to 189,000 pages, and Prime You appears in them in seven ways. None is a formal accusation against the company. All are useful.

1. The company introduces itself. On 26 November 2025, Prime Aviation Participações e Serviços, "known in the market by its trade name Prime You", petitioned the Federal Police chief to place itself at his disposal. It explained its two business models, shared ownership and asset management, said it had "more than 45" SPEs and acknowledged the connection that brought it there: Vorcaro's Viking is a quotaholder of one of its SPEs. In March 2026 it came back to say that it does not "lend" its name to anyone and that it had not been questioned since. proven (INQ 5026, item 2, pp. 116 and 117; item 545, pp. 1 to 3; item 582).

2. The police find a car. An investigative report of 18 November 2025 records that a seized Range Rover was held by PrimeAviation 4 Participações S.A., which "appears as an owner of Prime You in the federal tax register". PrimeAviation 4 is the former Fraction 044 and became DF & Range in March 2026. proven (INQ 5026, item 5, p. 717).

3. The jet and the helicopter that were to pay a law firm. A police report in PET 16662 describes a "Settlement and Payment-in-Kind Agreement" dated 19 May 2025, by which Viking would settle R$ 40 million of a R$ 50 million contract with a law firm by handing over "rights and interests" in two SPEs: "F24", owner of the Legacy 650 PP-NLR, and "F53", which the police identify as PT-PCT Administração de Bem Próprio. The firm stated publicly that the contract was never signed. The registry file adds what nobody had noticed: on the date of the offer, F53 had R$ 101 of capital; it was capitalised on 15 October 2025 and the helicopter only entered it on 25 November. What was being offered in payment was the shell. proven as to the documents (PET 16662, item 7, p. 161; item 9, p. 1; registry file no. 35.300.644.085; aircraft registry).

4. A witness speaks. In a statement filed in PET 15562, a witness says that "the company PRIME YOU in fact belongs to DANIEL VORCARO, but would be held in the name of the frontman MARCUS MATTA", and that Vorcaro "is the owner of house no. 4 on Ilha do Jorge in Angra dos Reis". It is the most quoted sentence about the company and the least proven: it is what one person told the police, with no document in the record confirming it. claim (PET 15562, item 54, p. 114).

5. The hundred names. PET 15198 contains a bank's January 2026 response listing the 100 individuals and entities whose bank secrecy was lifted, covering October 2020 to October 2025. Nine of them touch this dossier. An honest reading has to show both columns.

6. Two parliamentary inquiries want to know who flew. The Organised Crime inquiry approved, on 11 March 2026, Requirement 205, to the civil aviation authority, asking for the "ultimate beneficiaries linked to the aircraft registered PP-NLR and to the company Prime Aviation Participações", and Requirement 206, to Prime You itself, asking it to identify the passengers of the Legacy 650 since 1 January 2025. The joint inquiry into the social-security fraud asked the partners of Prime Aviation Táxi Aéreo, named as Artur Martins de Figueiredo, Rodolfo Garcia da Costa and Marcus Vinicius da Mata, for every flight, passenger and quotaholder of the same aircraft since 2015, plus the contracts "underpinning the use of the aircraft by Messrs Daniel Bueno Vorcaro, Sandra Habib or companies of the Prime You/Fractions group". Earlier requirements had already asked for the manifests of the G700 (PS-MGG) and of the Falcon 7X (PS-FST, this one owned by Viking, outside the SPE structure) in order, in the record's words, to "map the group's network of influence". Prime You asked Justice Gilmar Mendes to annul the disclosure order issued by the inquiry. proven (INQ 5026, item 543, p. 16; item 547, p. 5; PET 15556, item 247, p. 9; PET 15198, item 460, p. 5).

7. One member of Congress tells on another. A petition filed by congressman Henrique Vieira in March 2026 informs the Court that congressman Nikolas Ferreira had used, on at least two occasions in October 2022, the Phenom 300 registered PT-PVH, "belonging to the company Prime You, of which Daniel Vorcaro was a partner", on campaign trips, without declaring it to the electoral authority. The same petition states that Prime You "held the jet, Vorcaro's mansion in Brasília and other assets" and "was dissolved in September 2025, two months before Banco Master was wound up". A caution here: "dissolved" is the petition's word. The registry shows something else, and something more precise: Prime You was not dissolved; what changed in September 2025 was the name of three holding companies and, according to press reports, Vorcaro's departure from the ownership. claim as to the flights and the partner; proven as to what the registry records (PET 15556, item 190, pp. 2, 4 and 5; registry file no. 35.300.560.108).

And there is the backdrop the record preserves through press clippings filed by the police: the Botanique Hotel & Spa, in Campos do Jordão, whose corporate entity has Marcus Vinicius da Mata on its board, "is reportedly on offer for around R$ 150 million"; and "the most conspicuous of the aircraft used before his arrest by the owner of Banco Master, the Gulfstream G700, is for sale at a discount of more than R$ 100 million" (PET 15504, item 46, p. 253; PET 15556, item 1, p. 153). The G700 is PS-MGG. Its Brazilian registration was cancelled on 20 June 2025. Where it went, the Brazilian registry does not say; the press does (chapter 9), and the bank's liquidator went after it.

Chapter 9Alongside: the cases where Prime You appears without being the subject

The company is rarely the headline. It is the line beneath the headline: the name on the aircraft registration, the name on the deed. This section collects the reported episodes where that happened, separating what is in the Supreme Court record from what is only in the press.

A warning first: everything in this chapter that comes from the press is marked as a claim, even where the outlet attributes the information to a Federal Police report. What this dossier can do is cross each report against the registry: say which company that is, when it was incorporated, what it was called before and who runs it.

The house in Angra and the senator

In September 2026 the news site ICL Notícias reported that Senator Flávio Bolsonaro had celebrated his youngest daughter's birthday, in February that year, at a seafront mansion in Angra dos Reis with seven suites, a helipad and a jetty, "managed by Prime You". According to the report, the property is formally held by Real Estate Duo 004 Angra dos Reis Administração de Bens Próprios S.A., divided into four quotas, and one of the quotaholders, since 2022, is said to be a company belonging to the lawyer Willer Tomaz, a friend of the senator; another quota is said to be held by a fund linked to Maurício Quadrado. The lawyer replied that the house was used "solely on account of the friendship", with no payment, and denied any link between Vorcaro and the property. The senator's press office, Quadrado and Prime You's president did not respond. claim

What the registry adds: Real Estate Duo 004 Angra exists, is the former Fraction 014, was renamed after the town on 22 January 2021 and appears in the portfolio of Sharks FIP, a fund administered by Trustee DTVM, alongside Fraction 032 (the SPE chaired by Quadrado) and Fraction 024 (the PP-NLR). It is the machine of chapter 1, applied to a house. The registry entry read for this dossier, projection 5-B of Ilha do Jorge, is a different unit held by a different company of the group, Domus Realty; the report places the mansion in the condominium on the same island. Whether they are neighbours, the Angra registry will say. proven as to the company; the rest is press.

The Justice's jet and helicopter

In April 2026, Folha de S.Paulo and Metrópoles reported, based on civil aviation and air-traffic records, that Justice Dias Toffoli entered the executive terminal in Brasília on 4 July 2025, ten minutes before PR-SAD, "operated by Prime Aviation", took off for Marília, his home town; and that the same aircraft had flown Rio, Brasília and Marília on 21 March 2025, when a court had assigned security officers "to a Supreme Court authority" in that city. In September 2026, reports on a police file reproduced messages from March 2024 in which the former minister Fábio Faria tells Vorcaro that Toffoli had arranged lunch at the farm and asks whether he may use a helicopter "from Prime"; the reply attributed to Vorcaro is "Just ask at Prime, brother" and "I have a helicopter at Prime that's all mine". The Justice did not comment on the flights; on the police file, his office said he "has never held or kept funds abroad". Fábio Faria said he had never discussed cases with Justices. Prime replied that, "for reasons of contractual confidentiality", it does not disclose users. claim

What the registry adds: PR-SAD is a Phenom 300 whose owning company was incorporated in 2012 as PrimeAviation 3, first generation, and was renamed after the registration mark on 9 January 2014; the operator is Prime Aviation Táxi Aéreo. No report identified the registration of the 2024 helicopter; the group has four EC 155 B1s and one AS 350, and one of them, the PS-MAS machine, is precisely the one Prime You links to Viking in the court record. Whether the lunch flight took place is not known. In the Supreme Court record, what exists on Toffoli and Prime You is indirect: the Organised Crime inquiry summoned two of the Justice's relatives and the supervising Justice released them from attending, in a decision of 26 February 2026 (INQ 5026, item 376). proven as to the registry and the record.

The R$ 50 million contract and "we're very much enjoying the jet"

The payment-in-kind agreement of chapter 7 gained context in the press in September 2026: according to reports based on a police file, the R$ 50 million contract between the Barci de Moraes law firm and Viking is dated 12 May 2025, the lawyer Leonardo Palhares is said to have structured the proposal to settle R$ 40 million with quotas of the PP-NLR and the PT-PCT, and on 16 July 2025 someone from Prime You is said to have asked the firm's partner whether they were "enjoying the use of the quotas", and to have been told they were. Justice Alexandre de Moraes called the story "fanciful" and the inferences "absolutely false"; the firm said it had hired air-taxi services from several operators, "among them Prime You", and repeated that "nothing was signed". claim The registry, remember, shows that in July 2025 PT-PCT had R$ 101 of capital and no helicopter. proven

Who else flew

Metrópoles counted 161 take-offs by Prime You jets from Brasília between 2023 and September 2025, one every six days. Among the reported episodes: congressman Nikolas Ferreira and a pastor on the campaign tour of October 2022, aboard PT-PVH ("I didn't even know who the guy was", the congressman said); a shared Brasília–São Paulo flight on 28 August 2025 with Vorcaro and five politicians; and a flight to Maceió on 14 November 2025, three days before the arrest, with Justice Nunes Marques and his wife on board, their presence confirmed by his office. And one case that corrects the excess: Estadão reported that Justice Gilmar Mendes had flown on an aircraft operated by Prime You in February 2025; the company confirmed the trip, explained that the quota belonged to a businessman with no connection to Vorcaro, and the paper corrected the attribution. It serves as a reminder: forty-odd SPEs, each with up to four quotaholders, make for a great many owners; not every Prime aircraft is "Vorcaro's jet". claim

The partners who left and the aircraft that vanished

To the press, Prime You said that Vorcaro and Quadrado were partners until early September 2025 and "sold their interests back to the company itself"; the ICL site dates Vorcaro's entry to September 2021, and another report states that Nelson Tanure was a partner until 2023. On the G700: bought in June 2025 for R$ 538 million, the first of its model in Brazil, registered PS-MGG, US registration N739G; put up for sale in February 2026 for around US$ 80 million, with its last signal at Basel; then sold to an air-taxi company in San Marino, with R$ 280 million frozen at the request of Master's liquidator, and an agreement in a Florida bankruptcy court for the manufacturer to hand over the financing documents. Because the aircraft was held by the SPE and not by Vorcaro, it was not frozen at the outset. claim The registry confirms the frame: the SPE was incorporated with R$ 101, was capitalised with R$ 542.4 million via AFAC a month before the arrest, authorised the sale a month after it, and its Brazilian registration had already been cancelled back in June 2025. proven

Chapter 10The counterweight, which any reading must carry

  1. The lifting of bank secrecy reached neither the president nor any SPE. It reached Figueiredo, Freixo, Entre Investimentos, Aguetoni, Vorcaro, Quadrado and Viking. It did not reach Marcus Vinicius da Mata, nor Prime You, nor Prime Aviation Táxi Aéreo, nor a single Fraction.
  2. Prime You came forward to the Federal Police of its own accord, declared itself available, said it manages SPEs owned by third parties and states that it has not been questioned since.
  3. The "frontman" sentence is a witness statement, not a finding by any authority. No decision in the record adopts it.
  4. A private debenture issue to capitalise a bank is a lawful transaction. What the São Paulo court file shows is default and enforcement, not declared fraud. The statement that the guarantors were directors of Master comes from the claimant's pleading.
  5. Shared ownership is a legitimate business model, and one company per asset is standard practice in business aviation and yachting worldwide. Renaming a company is a lawful, everyday act.
  6. The law firm in the payment-in-kind agreement states that the contract was never signed.
  7. The two private individuals who appear in the Angra registry entry before 2022 have no connection to the case and are therefore not named.

What would settle the question, and does not yet exist in any public document: who advanced the AFACs that became hundreds of millions in capital (the SPEs' financial statements; the full deed of the 6th debenture issue); who the shareholders of the 86 companies are (the share ledgers); whether the 2019 Máxima credit line was ever drawn; who flew on PP-NLR, PS-MGG and PS-FST (the aviation authority and the parliamentary inquiries); where the G700 with serial number 87039 ended up (foreign aircraft registries); and whether the three guarantors were, in fact, directors of the bank (the Central Bank).

AnnexSources, method and limits

Commercial registry. 32 complete company files from JUCESP, the São Paulo commercial registry (free for registered users, one per company number) and 9 full-text documents (minutes and subscription forms, paid for). The file gives the name at incorporation and every "change of corporate name", with the date of the meeting. The full text gives who subscribed the capital.

Land registry. Full-text certificate of entry 10.707-A, First Registry Office of Angra dos Reis, 27.01.2026, digitally signed.

Aircraft registry. Public search of the Brazilian Aircraft Registry by registration mark, 17.09.2026: owner, operator, status, date of the last transfer.

Tax authority and securities regulator. Open data from the federal taxpayer register (current management of a closely held corporation, never its shareholders) and monthly fund portfolio and unit-price reports (2019 to 08/2026). The tax register keeps only each company's current name; it was by cross-referencing the tax number with sources that date the name — fund portfolios, the court record, the land registry — that the renamings surfaced.

The Supreme Court record. The 189,000 pages of Pet 15,556 and related cases, made public on 11.09.2026, read by lexical search; every citation carries the case, the item number and the page, in the same format as the rest of this site.

What was not done. No SPE financial statements were read (they are not public). No foreign aircraft registry was consulted. Neither the company nor the people named were approached; the right of reply that appears here is what they themselves put into the record. No personal data (tax numbers, identity numbers, street addresses, telephone numbers) has been transcribed, even where the documents contain it.
