The police summarise the statements "for textual reference", in orders that reproduce the speech in the third person. The reader does not hear the voice; he reads the clerk's summary. With that caveat, this chapter gathers what was said by the four people heard who are not in the WhatsApp group: the director, the manager, the lawyer and the concierge.

## The director

Ailton de Aquino Santos, the Central Bank's director of supervision, was heard as a witness on 25 May 2026. He began with the portfolios. He said he came to doubt the quality of the loans Master was selling to BRB in February 2025, because a bank "in a liquidity crisis" should not have been able to generate so much new credit. He commissioned the check not from Desup, "led at the time by Bellini Santana", but from the monitoring department. The result, in his words: "the loan portfolios did not exist", no Pix or wire transfer had reached the supposed borrowers; Master admitted two bad portfolios, of 23 and 26 December 2024, linked to associations in Bahia, and stood by the rest. Later, in a meeting with the originating company, its representative began by saying that it generated R$ 30 million a month, rose to R$ 50 million and, after an hour, "declared that the company generated six billion and that the portfolios were bad". Ailton ended the meeting and decided to notify the prosecution service. A sample of 300 transactions, out of 180,000, "found nothing". BRB's money stayed on Master's liabilities, without being passed on to the originator, "and nobody leaves six billion sitting idle".

About the two officials, he was cautious and then less so. He said he knew of their relationship with Vorcaro "in the ordinary dimension of any relationship between a bank supervisor and a banker", that he understood it as "republican and natural", and that "it is not usual for a supervisor to anticipate questions" or to guide the supervised. Asked whether, after learning of the payments, he had come to read the dealings differently, he answered that "looking at it in perspective" questions arise: "why officials Paulo Sérgio and Bellini Santana were so resistant to moving forward with the field work on the portfolios", and why Paulo Sérgio believed the irregularities to be "a mere operation that would be eliminated in a possible merger". "Was the debate between them really republican", he asked, and said it would be "reckless" to answer. Their argument, according to him, was always systemic risk: liquidation would contaminate other platform banks. He agreed that "there was no systemic impact whatsoever", but insisted that in April and May 2025 the fear was "genuine".

On 17 November 2025, he was precise. Vorcaro had asked for a meeting; he had offered Wednesday the 19th; "it was officials Paulo Sérgio and Belline who conveyed the request" and "repeatedly insisted" on bringing it forward; he gave in, and received Vorcaro at 12:30 pm on the Monday, in São Paulo, over Teams, with the two of them present. Vorcaro presented the transfer of control to an entity Ailton called "Fictos", with R$ 3 billion from "Arab investors", and said he needed to deliver the documentation that afternoon because he would be travelling. The two officials "said very little". Ailton, "internally", had already decided on liquidation; at 4 pm he called Márcio Contador, third in line in the department, and told him to be "at Banco Master's door the following morning"; the board voted that same day. Asked whether Belline and Paulo Sérgio knew of the liquidation, he answered "categorically not": "the witness and President Gabriel trusted no one at that moment". The reason, he said, was not a specific suspicion about the two, but "systematic leaks": "everything leaked, decisions, analyses, votes still at the draft stage". Not even the board knew.

He described the two as "professionals of the very highest quality and until then of unblemished reputation", Paulo Sérgio "acknowledged" as competent, to the point that the president had singled him out as the natural successor on the board; Belline, "of high international reputation", considered for director. And he closed with what "most deeply saddened him": in the statement of 7 January, Belline confirmed to him that he had received R$ 2 million; in the internal inquiry, "he stated that he had received four million".

## The manager

Márcio Contador Camargo, a Desup official since 2003 and technical manager of the team that followed Master, was heard on 11 June. He said that the department sent 31 letters to the bank between 2022 and 2024, "all answered"; that Vorcaro, in 2019, "arrived like a saviour" for the old Máxima; that Caixa Asset's refusal of Master's financial bills interrupted a R$ 10 billion funding plan. He said the department "was indeed deceived by the frauds", because "the numbers made sense, the transactions were registered at B3", and that "when there are partners in the crime, it becomes hard for the supervisor". It was he who drafted the fraud notifications to the prosecution service, in July and in November 2025, and both were signed by Belline, "without resistance".

On the farm contract, he explained the metadata: he is a lawyer, though he has never practised; Paulo Sérgio, a lifelong friend, sent him the draft to look over; the file "did not contain the buyer's details"; he made small corrections; he remembers a figure "of around R$ 1.8 million". On Zettel, he saw him once, in 2023, introducing himself as the lawyer of a Belo Horizonte company that had borrowed from Master. On Palhares, "he does not know him and has never seen him". On the payments to Belline, "he was completely unaware" and "the discovery surprised everyone". He knew that Belline gave talks on Black identity and "assumed he was not paid for those either".

He said he did not perceive, "to be honest", that the two had acted to benefit Vorcaro, and cited "two points that troubled the team". One: the two "strongly defended the acquisition of Banco Master by BRB", a position he then read as an attempt to reduce the impact on the system and which the technical team considered unfeasible because BRB is state-owned. Two: in the first quarter of 2024, the team had prepared a formal summons (*termo de comparecimento*) against Master and, "in the week the summons was to be drawn up, Paulo Sérgio asked to suspend it", converting it into a letter (*ofício*), an instrument "of lower rank"; the points were the same, and Paulo Sérgio e-mailed the team taking responsibility for the decision. "At that moment", he said, he was not suspicious; "looking back, he thinks differently". He also said that, on 17 November 2025, Ailton called him at 2 pm and took him to Galípolo's office, where he learned of the next day's liquidation and was instructed "not to comment on the fact to his two immediate superiors". And he added, unprompted, that in July 2019 he lent R$ 30,000 to Belline, "on account of a vulnerable financial situation", that he wrote the sum off as lost and that he got it back at the end of 2024; he reported the fact to Ailton, who told him that "other colleagues would also have lent".

## The lawyer

Leonardo Palhares was heard on 15 June, as a suspect, and spoke. He said he has been a partner at Varajo since 2020, that he chaired the Câmara Brasileira da Economia Digital for seven years and that he was a director of Super from mid-2024 until 12 December 2025, when he resigned. His version of the project is as follows. In October 2023, Fabiano Zettel, a friend since their university prep course, asked him to develop a study "together with Belline Santana", whom he did not know. Zettel said that Belline was "a person much admired both by him and by Daniel Vorcaro", that he "would retire soon" and that "there was perhaps the intention of hiring him or of continuing some work with him later". The figure, R$ 2 million, "was suggested to him"; Zettel informed him "that that amount was available", "without his being able to say for what reason". There was no written contract between Varajo and the companies that would fund it; "the funding commitment was undertaken verbally". Belline's e-mail came from Zettel. Belline asked for the signed letter "because he needed to notify the Central Bank", which "gave him a sense of security about the propriety" of it all.

He described the work: a study on youth in the urban periphery, completed in 2024, a podcast, and a second phase with a platform, Instagram and talks. Belline asked that the Chamber be left out of the second phase because Nubank was a member and he supervised it, a gesture Palhares read as "propriety". In February 2025, Belline appeared "very distressed"; they suspended; they resumed in May or June. In January 2026, Belline asked to leave.

On the money, he was direct: he issued invoices "to them" and received the amount with which he paid Belline; first from Moriah Asset, then from Super, as indicated by Zettel and Ana Cláudia. First phase, R$ 2 million in four instalments of R$ 500,000; second, "approximately R$ 700,000 or R$ 800,000", part of it to the company Inspiração. His own fee for legal work for the group was R$ 417,000 a month, "not specifically related to the project". On Super: "everyone knew it was a company whose assets belonged to him", Vorcaro, "an asset-protection structure"; his role as director "was nominal", with no access to the accounts. Asked why the source of the money was not in the contract, he answered that "that discussion was never had" and that he "cannot answer". He said that he "would not have taken part had he had any impression that the intention was otherwise", that he "never discussed with Belline any matter related to the Central Bank", and that the message about the "anxious friend" referred to the moment when Belline said he was too busy.

## The travel fixer

Léo Serrano was heard on 24 August as a witness. He said that Vorcaro and his family have been clients of his tourism and concierge company "for many years", without a fixed contract. In January 2024, Vorcaro asked "only for the guide service at the Orlando parks" for a guest who already had the rest; the VIP guide avoids queues and organises the itinerary; the service lasted "between five and seven days" and cost "approximately USD 38,000.00", paid by his company to the park and billed to Vorcaro. On Paris, "he did not recall at first"; Vorcaro asked for restaurant reservations, he passed them on to his European partner, and "as far as he knows, those reservations never even materialised".

There is also Paulo Sérgio's wife, heard on 7 July. She said that it was she who organised the Disney trip, hotel, flights and park days, and that her husband paid. Asked about Léo Serrano, "she does not know that person".

## What the four say together

None of the four claims to have seen a Central Bank decision being bought. The director says the two resisted investigating the portfolios and defended the merger; the manager says the same, with two examples, and also says he did not perceive favouritism. The lawyer confirms that Belline's money came from companies of Zettel and Vorcaro and that the figure came from above, but maintains that the project existed. The four, in different ways, describe a Central Bank that in 2025 did not trust its own heads of supervision, and two heads who did not know it. That is the portrait from outside. The one from inside is the next chapter.

> Reading rule number thirty-two: when a boss says "looking back" three times in the same statement, he is doing what every boss does after a disaster: reorganising his memory so that it points at someone else. The clerk writes it down; the reader discounts it.
