Petition 15.478 is a request for a bank and tax disclosure order (*quebra de sigilo*): 36 filings and 633 pages, of which 82 are the Federal Police's request (*representação*) and the rest, almost all of it, annexes and replies from banks. It is an ancillary proceeding, which decides no one's guilt, but the request that opens it is the first public document to show what the police read on the phones seized on 18 November 2025. This series has three chapters: what the police read on the phones, what the Supreme Court decided, and what the banks sent back.

The usual warning: these are excerpts chosen and transcribed by the accusing side to persuade a judge that it needs to see more. The defence was not heard beforehand, because the request is sealed and the federal prosecution service argued for "deferred adversarial proceedings" (*contraditório diferido*). None of those named has been indicted in the public filings.

## The internal name

The request, signed by police chief Janaína Pereira Lima Palazzo, of the Special Inquiries Division (Delegacia de Inquéritos Especiais) in Brasília, is addressed to Justice André Mendonça and carries a wrong date, "3 February 2025", which the context corrects to 2026: it cites the statement of the Central Bank's director of supervision, taken at the Supreme Court on 30 December 2025, and asks for data up to 13 December of that year. Among the annexes is the original request of October 2025 to the federal court, and there, in the header of the table of contents, appears the name the police gave the investigation before it became "Compliance Zero": "Operation Ostap Bender" (*Operação Ostap Bender*). Ostap Bender is the likeable con man of the Soviet novels by Ilf and Petrov, who knew "four hundred relatively honest ways" of taking other people's money. The police changed the name before launching the operation; the archive kept the original.

The police summary on the first page is the familiar one: "Banco Master, faced with the failure of its business model, coordinated a criminal scheme to sell and assign false CCBs to Banco de Brasília". What is new comes afterwards, under the heading "specific details of how the gigantic financial fraud was built".

## Tirreno's director

The first phone is that of André Felipe de Oliveira Seixas Maia, "alleged Director of Tirreno". On 5 May 2025, the day he "appears at Banco Master to sign contracts assigning Tirreno's credits to Banco Master, all on the same day", his girlfriend writes: "all this had better be worth it"; he replies "I know"; she: "your life is crazy right now"; he: "it's even giving me a chill down my spine"; she asks "is the deal that bad?"; he: "God help me" (*só por Deus*); she: "careful, love, some things aren't worth it". The police conclude that "there would be a financial gain from the deal, and it would have to make up for the risk". It is an inference drawn from a couple's conversation, and the only evidence cited that the director received anything; the request itself admits that "there was no payment for the credits generated by the company".

The same phone shows Henrique Peretto, a partner in Cartos, accompanying the director during the "roughly 8 hours" of signatures and sending him, on 17 April 2025, "the minutes of the extraordinary general meeting supposedly held on 2 December 2024". It is "the first date on which an official document of the company Tirreno appears in the communications between the two", two days after the commercial registry recorded the change of name. For the police, it is backdating.

## The partner and the invoices

The second phone is Augusto Ferreira Lima's. On it, according to the police, "Daniel Vorcaro turns to Augusto Lima to make sure he intervenes with BRB so that the financial institution makes the payments". The PGR, in the opinion that summarised the request, transcribed the messages: on 22 August 2024, "brother, give me the brb news later / I pushed today and they said it's stuck on the rate"; on 28 January 2025, "we need the portfolio urgently / brb, on the balance, hasn't selected the portfolio"; on 5 May 2025, "the team is stressed cause not even the tirreno contract has turned up / They're already making up theories / We need to send it right away", and "brother, if we don't send those ccbs and the contracts by noon we won't have any options left", to which Augusto Lima replies "I'm already up here". From January 2025, says the request, "the loan portfolios offered to BRB apparently came to be put together by Augusto Lima himself".

And there is the money. An employee connected to Augusto Lima sends him, on 29 August 2024, an invoice for R$ 3,665,962.47, "to be paid by Banco Master for services rendered by Terra Firme", Augusto Lima's company; on 7 April 2025, two more, for R$ 3,334,230.12 and R$ 3,665,962.47. The police see "payments that coincide with Augusto Lima's interventions with BRB", "in an amount that exceeded the mark of 3 million reais a month, an absolute nonsense". That is the hypothesis that justifies including the Terra Firme companies in the disclosure order. What the tax authority answered about one of them is in chapter 3, and it complicates the hypothesis.

## The "INFO - BRB" group

The third set comes from Daniel Vorcaro's phone, and it is the most direct. There is a WhatsApp group called "INFO - BRB", "of which only 3 members are part: Daniel Vorcaro, Alberto Félix and Ângelo", created "only in May 2025, when Banco Master began to be constantly pressed by BRB to produce the documents relating to the notes it had been acquiring since January". In it, according to the police, the director Ângelo Ribeiro produces the statement of Tirreno's linked account and the bank's owner complains about the balance: "the account doesn't add up", the PGR quotes. "The then President of Banco Master had already noticed the flaw in the document, requiring the final balance figure to be changed and teaching Alberto and Ângelo that the total amount shown should include remuneration." The police add that "the final statement, presented to the Central Bank, was fabricated with a third figure, which matches neither the 6,400 nor the 7,200". A statement requested, corrected and delivered with a third number: if the police reading is right, it is the tampering of a bank record under the controller's eyes.

Still in the same group, on 13 May 2025, the treasurer Alberto Felix answers a list of tasks from the boss and, under the item "proof of lien registrations" (*comprovantes de averbações*), writes: "that one's hard". For the police, "a logical conclusion, since these were non-existent credits and it would therefore be an impossible mission to validate them with the paying body". On 23 May, pressed to issue "CCBs en masse", the treasurer writes: "it's just that this business of the mandate clause and those ccbs has a certain, more delicate particularity to sort out". The mandate clause would be the customers' authorisation for Master to issue notes on credits raised by Cartos's correspondents, and "at no point was it sent to BRB or to the Central Bank". The police underline the date: "Alberto's review of the contracts is taking place only in May 2025 when, at least in theory, the contracts should already have been signed, since the deals date from January". Alberto Felix, in the series on Inquiry 5.026, described himself as a "mere agent" (*mero mandatário*). These messages are the police's answer to that defence, and the defence has not yet answered them.

Between the treasurer and Augusto Lima there is an operational flow: 26 March 2025, "Brb said it will try to settle 470mm tomorrow. Then we do 270mm in transfers, 200mm left in cash"; 10 April, "Dv asked to send another portfolio to brb"; 14 April, "tomorrow 2 people from BRB are coming to see the reconciliation of the financials", and the reply: "come here to align".

## The notebook

On the public bank's side, the police extracted the phone of its president, Paulo Henrique Costa, and seized the notebook of a director, whose name this site omits because she is treated as a source, not as a suspect. The notes are the strongest piece against BRB's management. Page of 11 July 2025: "President stated, again, that the portfolio purchases must be made, stating that those credits were verified and that if they are not made, Master will go under". And: "difficulty defending the purchase given the number of substitutions made in the last two weeks"; "The Tirreno contract needs to be terminated"; "the legal department's opinions since April/25 state that the assignments were split up to get 'around' the sign-off limits" (*regime de alçadas*). Page of 3 July: "Purchases: 90% outside policy". The police sum up: "the president ordered the purchases even with contrary legal opinions since April 2025", with "the clear intention of saving Banco Master".

The conversations between the president and the finance director, Dario Garcia Junior, go back to November 2024. On 19 November, "of a portfolio with a book value of R$ 179.6 million, only R$ 110 million were deemed eligible". On 26 November, the president writes: "I don't understand when they say there is no portfolio and a new one turns up"; the director replies: "neither do I". On 29 November, a contract for R$ 400 million of overdue credits is sent, signed, cancelled and resent on the same day, "three successive contracts within a few minutes", because "the settlement window closes at 5:30 pm". There is also the raising of the default limit "from 4% to 5%" and the use of "tranches" so as not to restart the approval cycle. The police call this "settling the operations at speed, with little concern for the risks". The more benign reading, that of two executives baffled by a supplier, is not in the record: "neither do I" is the line of someone who does not understand, not of someone in command.

## What the police say the statements said

The request cites three statements taken at the Supreme Court in December 2025, the only public source about them. The Central Bank's director of supervision is reported to have said, "categorically", that "had BRB's control mechanisms been working properly, the losses would have been avoided". BRB's president is reported to have stated "that Tirreno had not received any amount from Banco Master". And Daniel Vorcaro "declares himself fully aware of the operations with Tirreno, arguing that there was no crime owing to the supposed absence of loss", and "admits having cancelled the operation with Tirreno, and not having delivered the corresponding money either to Tirreno or to BRB". The police ask: "how can a company that received no amount at all and had its operation cancelled have committed itself to repurchasing portfolios worth 7 billion reais?". And they answer the no-loss argument with the Central Bank's audit of BRB: "the minimum loss to the public bank is 5 billion reais". The statements are not in the archive; what is known of them is what the police chose to quote.

> Reading rule number twenty-five: a phone extraction is a book from which the accusing side reads the pages it underlined. What it reads may be true and still not be the book.
