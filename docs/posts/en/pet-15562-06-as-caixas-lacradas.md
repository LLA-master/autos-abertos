The record of a search and seizure has two lives. The first lasts a morning and fits into search reports with photographs the public copy does not show. The second lasts months and is made of petitions about what was taken: who may see it, who may copy it, who may have it back. Petition 15.562 has both, and the second is not over yet.

## The morning of 4 March

In São Paulo, the team that went to Vorcaro's house carried two pieces of paper: the search warrant from this petition and the pre-trial detention warrant from Pet 15.556, both dated 3 March. The search record, handwritten, is illegible in the public copy. What can be read is the rest: at 5:22 am, the police chief sends the prisoner to the forensic medical institute (Instituto Médico Legal) for a forensic medical examination; at 9:20 am, the police clerk books him into custody at the regional headquarters, "by court order".

In Nova Lima, at Zettel's home, "no one was in the apartment to allow entry". The team called locksmiths, "who unanimously replied that it would not be possible to open the main and service doors because of the type of lock". A tactical team tried the main door, failed, and "broke down the service door", with two witnesses and two representatives of the prerogatives committee of the Minas Gerais Bar. The seizure record lists thirteen items: a sealed Sedex envelope addressed to a company care of Vorcaro's father; a document from an American bank in his name with a post-it note, "Henrique's address (Natalia's father) USA"; "a watch of apparent Rolex make"; an iPhone in a box which "leads one to believe that the box belongs to another device"; a promissory note and a cheque leaf for R$ 1.8 million, both marked "cancelled"; an orange diary from Zettel's law firm; folders of loan contracts, exchange agreements, property registrations; an iPad, a MacBook; and "a leather necklace with a blue stone", with certificate and proof of payment of R$ 190,000.

The item the police chief singles out in a separate report is "three loose A4 sheets": one "contains a handwritten note mentioning Directorates of the Federal Police ('DIP = Directorate of Police Intelligence' and 'Dicor = Directorate for Combating Organised Crime')", and two are "screenshots of a DIP system". In the home of the banker's brother-in-law, according to the police, there were screens from the intelligence system of the Federal Police itself. The report does not say from what date, nor what they showed.

In São Paulo, at Belline's home, four objects: two mobile phones, one in the bedroom, and two laptops, in the living room. The record notes that he "made available the access passwords to the equipment". That same morning he was served with the alternative measures from Pet 15.556: no contact, no entering the Central Bank, no leaving the municipality or the country, passport within 48 hours, suspension from office, ankle monitor.

Of Paulo Sérgio, Palhares, Ana Cláudia, Mourão, Marilson and Moriah, the public record carries no seizure records. Only the list of the 5th: executed.

## Who asked to come in

The requests for access to the file began on the same 4 March. Vorcaro, "for access to the entirety of the record relating to the launch of the third phase". Zettel, "considering that on this date precautionary measures of pre-trial detention and search and seizure were executed against him". Ana Cláudia, with "full willingness to cooperate with the courts". Palhares on the 5th, Belline on the 11th, the latter saying that he "will provide every clarification as soon as access to the present proceeding is granted". The justice granted them all on 27 March, with "temporary access" and "the restrictions inherent to the sealed nature" of the case.

Palhares was the first to complain about what he received. On 12 March, he recalled that he is a lawyer, that the warrant ordered the Bar Statute to be observed, and asked for "a mirror copy of the content extracted from his electronic devices, so as to allow access to his working material, unrelated to the object of the investigations", as well as to be notified so that he could follow the analysis. The justice sent the request to the police "for their information and action", and the letter went out on 30 March. There is no reply in the record.

On 30 March, Palhares came back, with a screenshot. He had been admitted as counsel in Petitions 15.562 and 15.556, but "was only granted individual access to certain filings entered in both proceedings, in a disorganised manner and without identification of page numbers". The filings from the two petitions "were made available 'mixed up'"; the system "does not allow more than one filing to be downloaded at a time" and "contains no numbering of the documents entered or reference to the date on which they were entered". The defence went from the justice's chambers to the registry, to the filing desk and to the ombudsman, and back to the chambers. It then asked for "full admission", with a seal only over "investigative measures that may happen to be in progress". There is no decision on that in this record.

## The laptop and the tax authority

Paulo Sérgio has the most insistent request. On 28 April, he "resubmits" in this record a request already made in Pet 15.556: on 24 April he had been notified by the tax authority (Receita Federal) about "pending issues in the tax-return cross-check" of his 2021 return, with twenty days to produce receipts for medical expenses. "The documentation requested, in particular the copies of the medical invoices, is stored on a laptop that was the object of the search and seizure." He lists what he wants back: a laptop "recently purchased by the petitioner to conduct his defence before the Central Bank's Internal Inquiry Committee", the Central Bank laptop "with access blocked", a mobile phone "with all his personal contacts" and an external hard drive.

On 21 May, he insists. He "has been waiting for almost two months", and Pet 15.556 had been, until then, "the only record whose existence was known to this defence". He had been summoned to give a statement on 27 May in Pet 15.504 and, by the federal comptroller (CGU), for an asset inquiry, within twenty days. He informed the police that "the taking of his statement is rendered unfeasible" without access to the devices. And he adds a fact: "almost three months after the seizure of the devices, no federal forensic report certifying the extraction of the data has yet reached the proceeding", something which "is observed to have already occurred in relation to media seized from other suspects". He also asks for a letter to the Central Bank to release his work e-mail and his Teams history, where there would be "a large volume of communications" that would demonstrate "the groundlessness of the suspicions".

The justice sent the file to the Prosecutor General's Office for comment on 22 May. On 29 May, Gonet advised refusal: the return is "unfeasible, at this moment", because "the interest in the seizure persists", and access to the e-mail and to Teams "conflicts with the suspension from the exercise of public office". On 13 July, the decision, one paragraph long: the claim "reproduces a request previously made, to be assessed within Pet 15.556"; repeating it "in a different proceeding, with no supervening new fact", is "improper duplication", "incompatible with the postulates of legal certainty"; "there is nothing to decide in this record". The Prosecutor General's Office acknowledged it the next day. Here, the laptop did not come back.

## The hard drive with a receipt

Belline asked for the return of his property on 18 May, eight days before his own statement. The argument: he handed over the passwords, the police have everything, "there is plainly no interest in keeping custody of the physical objects", and, in the alternative, that "a full mirror copy of the devices" be made, with a copy to the defence. The Prosecutor General's Office, on 23 June, advised against the return, "since the interest in the seizure persists", but, as to the copy, suggested hearing the police. The justice heard them. On 6 July, the police chief replied that "it is technically possible to produce an authentic copy of the computer data", with a hash, and that the media "belong to Belline Santana himself", which settles the question of privacy. With one condition: it would fall "to the lawyers and suspects to bear the burden of supplying new recording media (hard disk drives, HD), sealed in their original factory packaging and accompanied by the respective purchase receipt, in the quantity and with the specifications previously defined by the police officers". On 22 July, the justice granted it, "subject to compliance with the conditions required by the police authority". Of the purchase of the hard drive, the record says nothing more.

## Changes of lawyer

The rest of the record is the footnote of every long case. Vorcaro changed his defence twice: in April, a new power of attorney, signed in prison on 22 April, for two law firms; on 22 May, one of them transfers the brief without reservation to the other; on 25 August, a renewed request for registration. Zettel transferred his brief on 25 March and asked for the "removal from the docket" of his first lawyers. Ana Cláudia's lawyers withdrew on 20 May; on 4 September, a new lawyer asks for "full access to the progress of the inquiry with access to every filing". Maurício Quadrado, who is not a target of this petition, asked to be admitted on 17 August, "as well as in all related proceedings".

Up to 4 September 2026, the last filing, there is in this record no forensic report on any of the devices, no report of what the search in Ubatuba found, and not a word about the five members still missing from "A Turma". What the morning of 4 March produced is, as the seizure record says, under seal.

> Reading rule number thirty-nine: the search and seizure is the moment when the State knows more than it says; the six months that follow are the moment when the suspect discovers how much the State has not yet read. Between the laptop the tax authority is asking for and the forensic report that never came lives the question no petition asked head on: what is the point of seizing what is never examined? The official answer is "the interest persists". The honest answer is that nobody had the time.
