Bribery, in the Penal Code, is receiving an advantage "by reason of" one's office. The verb calls for a route: where the money left from, where it passed through, where it arrived. The police intelligence report (IPJ) in Petition 15.504 devotes half of its pages to that route, and it is the most concrete part of the file, because it deals with e-mails, contracts, payment spreadsheets and notary records, and not only with conversations. It is also the part in which the police infer the most. This chapter separates the two things.

## The e-mail that never arrived

The first message between Belline Santana and Daniel Vorcaro on the seized phone is from 18 October 2023, at 8:51 am. Belline says an e-mail has not arrived, confirms his personal Gmail address and asks for it to be resent. Two minutes after receiving the address, Vorcaro forwards it to his brother-in-law, Fabiano Zettel. The night before, at 9:18 pm, Zettel had sent Vorcaro a file called "Letter: Youth Inclusion Project" (*Carta – Projeto Inserção Jovens*). In the conversation between the two, Zettel asks "how Léo should send the e-mail", and Vorcaro dictates the text: "Dear Mr Belline".

The letter, the police show, proposes the commissioning of "a Technical, Economic and Social Study" and is signed by Leonardo Palhares, on behalf of a company called Varajo Consultores. It mentions neither Vorcaro nor Zettel. On 20 October, Belline tells Vorcaro, and not Palhares, that "everything was fine", and says he would take the letter of invitation to the head of internal affairs (*corregedor*) to "get the sign-off". When Belline's e-mail of acceptance to Palhares reaches Vorcaro's phone, forwarded by Zettel, the reaction of the two is "What a circus" (*Que circo*) and "Hahahahaha". For the police, this "confirms that the deal is a fictitious contract".

On 26 October, Zettel writes that on that day "Belline's first instalment would have to be paid". Vorcaro asks Zettel to check with Palhares "to make the payment", with reimbursement to follow. On 22 December, he instructs his brother-in-law to stop using Super, one of the group's companies. On 28 December, Zettel notes that "it is Belline's payday"; Vorcaro asks whether the money is coming out of the bank; the answer is that "obviously" it does not come out directly. In another passage, Vorcaro writes that "even indirectly" the payment must not be made by the bank. On 9 January 2024, the payment is still listed as pending; on 30 January, Zettel says he will pay "Bellini". On 2 October 2024, a message explains that part of R$ 760,000 sent to Palhares is meant for Belline. On 10 December, Vorcaro asks Ana Cláudia Queiroz de Paiva, who runs the finance side, through whom they are paying Belline; she answers that she pays Palhares, and Palhares pays Belline. On 2 January 2025, a list of pending items carries Belline's name next to R$ 750,000. In September 2025, a list of "fixed monthly expenses" records Belline at R$ 500,000. One of the spreadsheets calls the whole set of payments the "friends'" payments.

The police also show a note on the phone with a list of names followed by the word "Bacen", among them "Bellini Bacen", to prove that the variant spelling is the same person; and a message from the secretary, on 6 June 2025, announcing the arrival of Paulo Sérgio and "Bellini" at the office, with Vorcaro's order to stop the building work and get everyone out.

Hence the IPJ's diagram, in figure 104: the money leaves group companies, passes through Palhares and his Varajo, and reaches Belline. In the first phase, the document says, through Moriah Asset and Super; later, through Palhares. On 4 February 2025, Palhares himself writes to Vorcaro: "I was with that friend today, the one I commissioned a study from. It's under control, but he seemed more anxious than usual". The police read "friend" as Belline.

## Paris

The second item on Belline's list is a trip. In September 2025, a contact of Vorcaro's in Europe asks about the "preparations to receive a guest" and suggests lunch, dinner and a guided tour in Paris. Vorcaro does not reply that day. On 11 September, he presses his travel fixer, Léo Serrano, for the arrangements; the European contact comes back, and Vorcaro asks for a dinner at Lapérouse, a lunch at Loulou, "good wines and food", and sends Belline's contact details. Travel records consulted by the police indicate that Belline and his wife left Brazil for Paris on 9 September and returned on the 15th. There is, in the IPJ, no proof that the dinners took place. In August 2026, in his statement, Belline would say that he had never been to either restaurant. That is for chapter six.

## Orlando

With Paulo Sérgio, the list has two items. The first is Disney. On 30 January 2024, he sends Vorcaro the itinerary of a family trip. Vorcaro forwards it to Léo Serrano saying he needs "to get a guide" for those travellers; Serrano asks for the name; the answer is "Paulo sergio souza". On 5 February, Paulo Sérgio says thank you: "Léo just messaged me", and says they are boarding. The police infer that Vorcaro "would have had a hand in making the trip possible". What the trip cost, and who paid, only appears in August 2026, when the travel fixer gives his statement: about US$ 38,000 in guides, billed to Vorcaro. That is a matter for chapter five.

## Muzambinho

The second item is the farm. On 15 January 2020, Zettel sends Vorcaro a Word file with the draft of a purchase and sale agreement for a rural property in the south of Minas and asks Vorcaro to send it to someone, to "finalise the wording". The sellers are Paulo Sérgio and his brother; the buyer's name is left blank. The police underline Vorcaro's role: the final buyer "would not even have had contact with the sellers", and the draft passes through the banker. The file's metadata say it was created by Márcio Contador Camargo, a Central Bank official posted to Desup, a detail the police record and which Márcio himself would explain in June.

The rural environmental registry shows the property transferred, on 6 October 2021, to Pipe Participações, a company of Zettel's. The draft speaks of R$ 4.8 million; the public deed drawn up on 13 January 2021, in Juruaia, records R$ 3 million. "Only the value of the instalments went into the deed", the IPJ concludes. The Muzambinho notary registry would confirm to the police, in July 2026, that the deed of 13 January 2021 conveyed the property to Pipe "and as a capital contribution" (*integralização de capital social*). Pipe, the police write, "may have been used merely as an intermediary" (*interposta pessoa*). Note the verb: may.

In January 2026, Paulo Sérgio had told the Central Bank that he had sold for R$ 4.5 million. In July, he would tell the police that the total was R$ 4.8 million, with an addendum in 2022 because of the rise in coffee prices. Three figures for the same farm, none of them the one in the deed. His explanation is in the last chapter; here, it is worth recording only that the difference between the contract and the deed, in rural Brazil, is so common that it has a name, the "drawer contract" (*contrato de gaveta*), a side agreement, and that the police do not assert that it hides a bribe. They assert that the sum of the elements "reinforces the atypical nature of the relationship".

There is a second property that is not in the IPJ and that the police would only find in the inquiry: a house in Guaxupé, built by Paulo Sérgio next to his own residence, with a gym inside, sold on 30 January 2024 to a company called Noah Empreendimentos, which the police link to Zettel and whose administrator was Paulo Sérgio's brother. The house remains empty and the family still uses the gym. We shall come back to it.

## The operators

The IPJ's third conclusion is about who moves the money. Zettel is described as "directly responsible for the movement of funds, the drawing up of fictitious contracts and the passing on of payments", through "SUPER, PIPE Participações and possibly other legal entities under his management". Palhares "emerges as the formal intermediary" (*interposta pessoa formal*), whose company would serve "to issue apparently simulated documents". Ana Cláudia "takes part in the acts of laundering" by carrying out the transfers. For the three of them, and for Belline and Paulo Sérgio, the police ask for the lifting of bank secrecy, and list the companies: six linked to Palhares, among them the Câmara Brasileira da Economia Digital, the digital-economy trade body he chaired, and Super itself, of which he was a director between 2024 and 2025; 28 linked to Zettel, among them a church, a car park and a technology company whose current managing partner, says the footnote, is Vorcaro.

The disclosure order was requested, and decided, in another petition, whose data, in July 2026, had still not reached the police system. That is why, in this petition, the money trail is made of messages that talk about money, and not of money. The distinction seems subtle and is decisive: a WhatsApp spreadsheet with the name "Bellini" and the figure 750,000 proves that somebody wrote that. What was paid, when, by whom and to whom, is in bank statements that Petition 15.504 does not have.

## What is known and what is supposed

To close, a mental table. Known, by document: that there was a letter of invitation from Varajo to Belline, in October 2023; that Belline received payments, he declares them himself, and that Varajo made them; that the farm was sold to Pipe by a deed of R$ 3 million; that there was a trip to Disney with a guide arranged by one of Vorcaro's fixers; that there was a trip to Paris with a reservation requested by Vorcaro. Supposed, by police inference: that Varajo was a front; that "What a circus" means simulation; that Pipe was an intermediary; that the dinners in Paris were paid for. Between the two columns lies the work of the inquiry, and that is what the next chapters describe.

> Reading rule number thirty: money that passes through three companies before reaching someone may be laundering or may be the bookkeeping of a disorganised family group. The difference is not in the route; it is in the motive. And the motive is what the police do not yet have in a bank statement: they have it in messages.
