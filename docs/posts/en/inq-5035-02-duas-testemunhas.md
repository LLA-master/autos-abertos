Everything that Inquiry 5.035 produced by way of evidence of its own, up to August 2026, is in two statements taken on the same day, 12 February 2026, by video conference, by police chief Ramon Santos Morais, and in two notarial records that the witnesses brought with them. This chapter recounts them in order, because the order matters: the two did not know each other, were approached one day apart, by different people, and tell versions that fit together without coinciding.

## The councillor

Rony Gabriel, city councillor in Erechim, was heard in Porto Alegre, as a witness, "under the obligation to tell the truth". He said that on 20 December 2025 a man who introduced himself as André Salvador approached his aide. The message is recorded in a notarial record drawn up at a notary's office in Erechim on 9 January, and this site transcribes it as it stands: "We are monitoring the networks for Flavio Bolsonaro too and I found Rony's profile in those posts. But the subject here is another one. We are doing reputation management and crisis management work for a big executive. And we have been hiring profiles that take a stand to help us in this political dispute we are waging against the system."

To find out what it was about, the aide had to sign, on 27 December, a "Non-Disclosure Agreement" with the company UNLTD Network Brazil Ltda., whose object was "the strategic project in question, for now named: PROJECT DV". The contract is in the record. Its clause 8 says that a breach subjects the party "to the payment of a non-compensatory penalty in the amount of R$ 800,000.00 (two hundred thousand reais)". Eight hundred thousand in figures, two hundred thousand in words. The councillor found the amount "funny, comical"; the site finds the carelessness revealing. The chosen forum is Pelotas, in Rio Grande do Sul. The signatory for UNLTD signed on 29 December, two days after the aide.

On 29 December, a Monday, there was a meeting over Google Meet. "Then, yes, he makes it clear what it is about", the councillor testified: "he said it concerned Mr Daniel Vorcaro of the Banco Master case". The police chief asked twice whether the name was spoken, and the answer was yes, "only in that meeting, after signing the contract". The thesis the client wanted to see spread: "That the Central Bank was wrong, that the Central Bank would be getting it wrong. That the Federal Court of Accounts should intervene, or something to that effect, or would come to intervene." The councillor asked for "some model from someone who had already done it", and received by WhatsApp, during the meeting, six links: five videos and one post from a gossip page, "all with the same narrative that the TCU went on the attack because the Central Bank may have got it wrong". The links are in the notarial record; this site does not open them or name their authors, because the only evidence that they were "models" is the statement of the person who received them. On payment: "he made it clear that it was a matter of millions, but I did not get as far as learning the amount". They offered flights to São Paulo, "to talk directly with the interested party". On that same 29th, the aide sent in writing "the REFUSAL to take part in the above project". The reply: "No worries, boss, thanks for getting back, I'll let the client know."

What made the councillor speak out, days later, was a news item. Around 4 January, he read that a minister of the Federal Court of Accounts had issued an order "that could un-liquidate Banco Master", to be taken to the full court on 21 January. "I realised that the narrative being commissioned was corroborating that news." And he gave his reading, which the police chief left on record as an opinion: "they were hiring crisis management, but not to take the heat off a bank, rather to influence a decision of the Federal Court of Accounts, which could serve as a basis for the judiciary to give a 'stroke of the pen' (*canetaço*), un-liquidate the bank, compensate Daniel Vorcaro, end the investigations". It is the inquiry's hypothesis, spoken by a witness. It is not proof of it.

## The journalist

Juliana Moreira Leite, journalist and presenter, 1.6 million followers, was heard on the same day, accompanied by lawyers and by a notarial record drawn up in São Paulo the day before. She began with what she did not know: "I cannot say it was Daniel Vorcaro, because in fact I did not have that, I never got to sign a contract, or even see a contract". On 21 December, someone who identified himself as an influencer agent wrote to her on Instagram: "I would like to know who I am speaking to in order to close an advertising deal. I have an interested client." She gave her WhatsApp number and received two voice messages, which the notary transcribed. "I have a client who needs to publicise several articles, OK? Political, financial sphere, and so on." And: "the client wants to do a contract of at least three months, right? But it can be month by month too. With eight videos a month, OK? Two a week. Then, for me to go deeper into the topic, I need a non-disclosure agreement."

Then came a news article, "questioning why the Central Bank had liquidated Banco Master", with the comment "this one's already gone", which she understood as "the other influencers had already spoken". "The moment he sent that, I deduced it." She replied: "Júnior, I am not available to post articles about Banco Master." Reply: "no worries, thank you very much, that's it." She then did the opposite of what was commissioned: a video explaining "how the liquidation of a bank happens" and ending, according to her statement, with the line "for the right money, Daniel Vorcaro even becomes the victim". She sent the voice messages to a friend, a TV presenter, who prepared a report on the subject; "unfortunately that report was dropped, they received an interim order". Later, on returning from a trip, she called the councillor and handed everything to the columnist at O Globo, "who had already noticed that this militia existed, she just did not have the proof".

The police chief asked whether she could point to profiles she suspected had been hired, and she cited names, with the caveat that "it cannot come out anywhere publicly that I said it". The site respects the caveat for another reason: they are a witness's suspicions about people the record does not investigate. What she stated from her own knowledge was the pattern: "all of them posted on exactly the same Friday, people who do not talk about Banco Master". And what she stated from hearsay: "there is one of them who tells a friend of mine, wow, I signed, but publicly said no". On "Project DV", she was explicit: "I know it is called Project DV and Daniel Vorcaro was in there, because Rony Gabriel said so, but I cannot say it, because I did not get that far".

## What the two statements prove

Read together, they prove three things and do not prove a fourth. They prove that there were, in December 2025, at least two approaches to content creators, by different people, with the same format: unnamed client, confidentiality before the subject, a three-month contract, eight posts a month, an agenda against the Central Bank. They prove that a company called UNLTD Network Brazil put the expression "PROJECT DV" in a non-disclosure agreement. And they prove that posts with the same content appeared in series in the last days of December, which police intelligence confirmed on its own account by listing 22 profiles with posts "at close intervals", many deleted afterwards.

What they do not prove is who the client was. The only nominal link between the campaign and the banker is the councillor's account that the name was spoken in an unrecorded video conference, after the contract was signed, and the acronym "DV" in a document signed by a marketing company. The journalist, honestly, said she deduced it. The police, in the opening order, wrote that "the contracts were allegedly offered in the name" of Thiago Miranda's agency, and that is, so far, the firmest end of the thread: an agency, a payment traced by the press, a businessman who, as chapter 3 tells, asked for access to the record even before the inquiry existed at the Supreme Court. Between the agency and the "big executive", the inquiry has not yet placed anyone.

There is a detail of form that is worth more than many pages. Asked whether "they directed all the content you were supposed to say", the councillor answered: "They had a script, but it could be said in whatever way I thought best, each one in their own style." Advertising with a script and freedom of style is the description of any influence campaign, lawful or not. What turns it into a crime, if it does, is not the method but the purpose the opening order attributes to it: "hindering the progress of the investigations". That purpose, in the statements, appears as the witnesses' deduction and as a reading of a news item about the TCU. It is little for a criminal case and a lot for public opinion. The inquiry exists to turn the one into the other, and it has not yet done so.

> Reading rule number twenty-three: when two witnesses tell the same story by different routes, what they have in common is evidence and what only one of them has is an account. Here, the method is common; the client's name is an account.
