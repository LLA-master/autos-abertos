An archived case is, in theory, a dead case. Constitutional complaint RCL 88.121 was archived on 10 February 2026 and, up to 19 August of the same year, received more than a hundred filings. This last chapter is the inventory of what arrived at an address that officially no longer existed, and of what that says about the place the complaint came to occupy in the case: that of the only public proceeding in which the injured bank speaks.

## January: the leak

On 4 January 2026, Vorcaro's defence returned to the case because of a document that was not in it. In the last days of 2025, the press had reported a submission by the Central Bank to the federal audit court (TCU), "which should have been confidential", saying that "on 17 November 2025 there was a new communication to the MPF over indications of the crimes of misappropriation of funds and fraudulent management", concerning "structured operations in the amount of R$ 11.5 billion". The reports linked those operations to funds administered by REAG. The defence protested: the vote that ordered Master's liquidation is classified by the Central Bank itself "at the secret grade of confidentiality" for eight years; the operations were loans to companies "between December 2023 and July 2024"; the Central Bank had supervised them "as early as 2024" without pointing to fraud; and "no sanctioning proceeding was ever opened by the BCB against Daniel Vorcaro", with a clearance certificate "issued on 2 January 2026". It asked that the Central Bank explain the communication and that "any investigation opened" on the strength of it be sent to the Supreme Court, "subjecting it to the same treatment given to Operation Compliance Zero". The reader of the series on Petition 15.198 knows that a second phase of the investigation, about funds, would be born that month. The request of 4 January was never decided in this complaint.

## January: the senator's letter

On 29 January, the president of the INSS congressional inquiry, Senator Carlos Viana, wrote to Justice Toffoli asking for authorisation to bring Vorcaro to testify "as a witness" on 5 February, and "that attendance be made compulsory". The letter is the frankest document in the archive on the friction between Congress and the Supreme Court's case law. "There has been, on the part of some Justices of the Court, a significant misunderstanding as to the scope of what was decided in ADPFs 395 and 444." Those actions "at no point refer to the case of parliamentary investigations". "Orality is a deep mark of Brazilian congressional inquiries." "Witnesses may remain silent, but Parliamentarians have the right and the duty to ask." "Without witnesses present, there is no possible questioning." And it recalls that "the only collegiate decision of the Supreme Court in a case about congressional inquiries came down to a tie". There is no reply to that letter in the record of the complaint. There is, in another proceeding, the answer that the new reporting justice of the inquiry would give the organised-crime congressional inquiry a month later: attendance optional. The senator's letter is, therefore, the losing argument, written before it lost.

## February and March: the new reporting justice

On 23 February, the president of the Court, Edson Fachin, ordered the complaint redistributed, with no stated reason, as had happened with the inquiry ten days earlier. On 27 February, Justice André Mendonça, "considering the arrival of supervening petitions", took the case out of the archive. On 13 March, he decided what to do with the first of them: BRB had asked for something, the liquidators of Master, Will Financeira and Banco Pleno, the Central Bank and the MPF had been heard, and the reporting justice ordered everything detached from the file "so that it may serve to instruct a new PET to be distributed to these chambers on grounds of prior assignment" (*por prevenção*). That is how, by detachment, at least one of the numbered petitions in the archive was born. But BRB did not stop writing to the complaint.

## BRB speaks

What follows is the voice that was missing from the other series. Between March and July 2026, the state-owned bank filed five petitions, all signed by its superintendency and its legal directorate, and the Federal District filed one. Together, they form the buyer's version.

On 16 March, BRB asked for the preservation of quotas in three offshore funds, one in the Cayman Islands and one in the Bahamas, which Master "contractually undertook to transfer to BRB" as part of the "asset recomposition" for the Tirreno portfolios, whose "impact on its assets" the bank estimates "at approximately R$ 12 billion". The contracts were signed, but "the formal registration of the transfer of the holdings was not completed", and Master's liquidation "significantly altered the context". BRB asked that the liquidator be ordered to sign what was missing, or that the quotas be liquidated and the money deposited with the court. On 30 June, it repeated the request with a new fact: on 26 May, the Supreme Court of the Bahamas had recognised the Brazilian liquidator "as foreign representative" of the Master conglomerate in that country, and "the liquidator's action in the Bahamas targets, among other structures", precisely one of the funds BRB says it bought.

On 2 April, the bank asked that "all goods, funds, assets, rights, credits and financial flows offered within any cooperation agreement" (*colaboração premiada*) "remain reserved, segregated and judicially bound", with "precedence for the right of the injured party". On 5 May, it was specific: a newspaper column had reported that Vorcaro's cooperation "may involve payment of a fine and the definition of criteria for the allocation of assets", and BRB set its "minimum recomposition threshold": "the amount currently provisioned reaches R$ 7,355,253,036.76". Seven billion, three hundred and fifty-five million. It is the third number in this series for the same loss, and it is the injured party's own, in May 2026.

On the same 5 May, the gravest petition. BRB reported "facts of extreme gravity, with unequivocal criminal contours": the "deliberate redirection of financial flows linked to the Credcesta product". The scheme, according to the bank, would consist "of informing public bodies of the supposed 'termination' of rights, followed by the change of the pass-through accounts and the internalisation of the amounts by structures linked to PKL One Participações S.A. and Banco Pleno S.A.". BRB points to Augusto Ferreira Lima, "controller of Banco Pleno and historically linked to the creation and expansion of the Credcesta product", as the "possible command centre, economic beneficiary or organiser", and asks that the flows be segregated under the liquidator's control, that the petition go to the MPF and that PKL disclose "the current destination of the financial flow of the entire Credcesta product". The bank reports having filed a civil action and a criminal complaint on the same subject. It is an accusation by a state-owned bank against a private bank, made in an archived case, and this site reports it as what it is: one party's version, with no decision.

## The accounting request

On 20 July, BRB asked for the "setting aside of the ordinary effects of CMN Resolution 4.966/2021", the rule that requires banks to provision for expected losses, over "the portfolios originating from Banco Master", "until the existence, ownership, performance, destination of the flows and effective recoverability of the respective assets have been sufficiently established". The petition gives the bank's arithmetic: between July 2024 and October 2025, "loan portfolios from Banco Master and Will Bank totalling R$ 22,989,790,491.43"; the Tirreno assets "reached the approximate amount of 12 billion"; in exchange, BRB received funds and a wholesale portfolio of "approximately 10 billion", and "2 billion were not covered", recognised as a loss and excluded from the request. On 22 July, the Federal District, through its attorney general, joined in, with the argument of the one who pays the bill: "the loss recognised on the institution's balance sheet does not stay there. It becomes a need for capital, and the need for capital becomes a fiscal effort by the controlling entity". BRB pays "approximately 210,000 local public servants", runs "25 social programmes" and holds "a portfolio of judicial deposits of around R$ 30 billion". The capital recomposition already authorised has a "predetermined quantitative limit", of "up to 16% of Net Current Revenue", and an "artificially inflated" provision could exceed it.

The reader should note what is being asked: that the Supreme Court, in an incident within a constitutional complaint, suspend for one bank the application of a prudential rule of the National Monetary Council. There is no decision on this in the record this site has read. There is the reminder, in the petition itself, that the December interim order had barred the Central Bank from complying with orders "that do not come from this Supreme Federal Court". A sentence written to protect the investigation became, eight months later, the ground on which the investigated victim asked the Court to regulate its accounting.

## August: the last to arrive

On 19 August 2026, a lawyer under investigation in a 2017 operation, with no declared connection to Master, asked to join the complaint. He recounted that, in his cases in São Paulo, the prosecution service had been asking for suspension and transfer to the Supreme Court "in view of Constitutional Complaint 88.121 and Petition 15.198", because of "a possible connection with Operation Compliance Zero", and that a court in Minas Gerais had tried his case anyway. He asked for the suspension of all his cases, in five states. The site does not know whether the connection exists; it knows that, nine months after a folder moved a case from one building to another, people who never appeared in the case are trying to get in through the same door. That is what constitutional complaint RCL 88.121 came to serve for, and that is why it will not close.

> Reading rule number twenty-one: the archive, at the Supreme Court, is a state of mind. An archived case with jurisdiction recognised goes on being the place where everything that fits nowhere else is asked for.
