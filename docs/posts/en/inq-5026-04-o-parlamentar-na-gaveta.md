Inquiry 5.026 did not move up to the Supreme Court because someone proved that a member of Congress was involved. It moved up because someone said there might be. The difference is the whole story of this chapter, and it is documented in a three-page order, written at 7:21 pm on a Wednesday, by a judge who was not the judge of the case.

## The constitutional complaint

The starting point is a petition that Daniel Vorcaro's defence filed with the STF, docketed as constitutional complaint (RCL) 88.121, with Justice Dias Toffoli as reporting justice. The federal prosecution service (MPF), which read it, sums up the argument: "during the execution of precautionary measures at one of his residences, a document was seized that supposedly had a connection to an authority, in this case a federal congressman, holding privileged venue before the Supreme Federal Court". The document, still according to the MPF, made "reference to a federal congressman from the state of Bahia" and to facts of the so-called Operation Overclean, which runs at the Supreme Court under another reporting justice, Justice Nunes Marques. The defence asked that the investigation be suspended and sent to the STF.

The MPF found the device "procedurally atypical and unusual": "the defence intends, on the basis of a document that was found, to involve its own client in criminal facts, even though that supposed participation is neither known nor under investigation". In other words, the suspect was telling the Supreme Court: there is a congressman in my case, take it away from the first-instance judge. For the prosecution, it was a "flagrant attempt to force the jurisdiction of the STF". The congressman's name and the description of the document, an option agreement for the purchase of a property, are in the record of the constitutional complaint, which has its own series on this site; here it is enough to say that the Supreme Court's decision states that it does not know whether the fact has any connection to the congressman's mandate.

On 2 December 2025, the Supreme Court sent a letter to the Brasília judge asking for information. On the 3rd, Toffoli issued an order in the constitutional complaint: he granted the Federal Police access and wrote that, "until further consideration of the request", "new steps and measures must first be submitted to the scrutiny of this Supreme Court", "including in other related investigations", because, "faced with an investigation supposedly directed at persons holding privileged venue by reason of office, as indeed already reported by the formal press, the jurisdiction of the constitutional court is settled". Note what the justice did not do: he did not call up the record, he did not suspend the investigation. He asked that new measures pass through him.

## The duty judge

On the night of that same 3 December, the presiding judge of the 10th Federal Court, Antonio Claudio Macedo da Silva, signed a decision that opens with a confession of method: "this magistrate is not the judge presiding over the case, having knowledge of and access to the record only on this date, owing to the holidays of the eminent Substitute Federal Judge". He read the Supreme Court's letter and concluded, "subject to the better judgment to be made by the eminent Reporting Justice", that "the mere reference to an authority holding privileged venue by reason of office must already call for caution", and that "the most appropriate course is to send the record of this Police Inquiry, as well as the records of all related precautionary measures, for the analysis of the distinguished Reporting Justice". He ordered the Federal Police "to perform no further investigative act", sent the inquiry and the four precautionary proceedings, detention, search, asset freeze and disclosure order, to the STF, and closed the cases on his court's books.

The decision has a paragraph that is almost a manifesto, and that explains the choice: "the preservation of constitutional jurisdictions must not be subordinated to any haste to investigate any facts whatsoever, for the recent past of criminal jurisdiction in Brazil shows that rushed judicial action ends up nullifying every institutional effort to fight crime, as well as wasting resources and bringing the Judiciary into discredit before society". Anyone who knows the last decade knows which recent past he means without naming it.

## The motion for clarification

Two days later, on 5 December, federal prosecutor Gabriel Pimenta Alves filed a motion for clarification (*embargos de declaração*), and the text is the frankest document in the inquiry about what the prosecution thought of all this. The decision, he writes, "was issued of the court's own motion, at 7:21 pm on 03/12/2025, without any prompting from the suspect's several and very active defence lawyers", by a judge "who is not the judge responsible for the case". It "suffers from serious obscurities and contradictions, capable of creating procedural turmoil, besides defying a determination of the eminent Justice Dias Toffoli". The justice, the MPF argues, "did not order the suspension of the investigations and the referral of the record to the STF, but merely established that new steps and precautionary measures must first be submitted to the scrutiny of this Supreme Court". The judge "relied precisely on the content of the said Complaint, but ended up concluding in a manner completely dissociated, contradictory and obscure".

The MPF also delimits the object of the inquiry with a precision that serves as a summary of the whole series: "the investigation by the Federal Prosecution Service and the Federal Police of the Federal District is restricted to the possible crimes committed to the detriment of Brasília's public bank, BRB, within a delimited period, in the first months of the current year". The document about the congressman from Bahia "has no connection whatsoever with the investigation"; if it is evidence of another crime, "the proper measure is to extract it and send it to the reporting justice" of Overclean. And it predicts: the complaint "will probably be dismissed without examination of the merits". It asks that the suspension and the referral be revoked, "avoiding unnecessary procedural turmoil, with the record being sent away only to possibly return afterwards", and, "considering the wide publicity given to the decision under challenge", authorisation to publish the motion, "to avoid disinformation".

There is no decision on the motion in the record this site has read. What there is, is the result: the record went up. The copies of the Brasília proceedings that today make up Inquiry 5.026 at the STF carry, in the header of each one, the date of 18 December 2025. The MPF predicted the turmoil; the turmoil became the new address.

## The Supreme Court in January

At the STF, Toffoli kept to what he had said on 9 December in the constitutional complaint, as the PGR recalls: "all administrative, civil and criminal decisions issued so far in relation to all the suspects remain in force, for now, including the restraints on assets and funds", with "the investigations continuing under the immediate supervision of this Supreme Federal Court". On 24 January, at the request of the police and with the PGR's opinion, he ratified the first-instance measures and authorised "the continuation of the steps relating to the extraction and analysis of the seized material", because there were witness interviews scheduled for 26 and 27 January. On the 26th, he recorded that "all the suspects in this inquiry are duly represented by retained counsel" with access to the record, and said that a request from the police had already been dealt with "in the record of Pet. 15.198, to which it relates more closely", "there being no need for a duplicate investigation". On 12 February, at the request of defence teams that could not obtain the forensic reports on the devices, he ordered the Federal Police to send to the STF "in full, the content of the devices and of other media", the forensic reports and "other elements of evidence already documented", "so that the provisions of Binding Precedent 14 can be complied with".

That is Toffoli's last decision in the inquiry. From 19 February onwards, all of them are by Justice André Mendonça. The record of Inquiry 5.026 does not contain the redistribution order, which is in Petition 15.198, dated 13 February, with no stated reason. This site records what the record records: thirteen days after the redistribution, on 26 February, the new reporting justice granted a safe-conduct, for the congressional inquiry committee on organised crime, to two summoned persons named José Eugênio Dias Toffoli and José Carlos Dias Toffoli. The record contains nothing linking the justice to those two summoned persons beyond what the committee itself stated when it summoned them, and this site suggests nothing beyond what is written in the filings.

## What remained of the constitutional complaint

The reader may ask what became of constitutional complaint 88.121, the proceeding that pulled everything along. The answer is in the archive: it is one of the fifteen published proceedings, with 404 filings and 4,301 pages, and it deserves a series of its own. What this chapter can say is its effect: a document about a congressman, found in a search, caused an inquiry about two banks to leave the Federal Courts of the Federal District and come to run before a single justice, without anyone, in the record this site has read, having asserted that the congressman had any connection to BRB or to Master. What the Supreme Court did with the complaint, and what the Central Bank sent to it, is the next series. The MPF called it an attempt to force jurisdiction. The duty judge called it prudence. The Supreme Court, at least until February, called it supervision. All three may be right at the same time, which is the definition of an institutional problem.

> Reading rule number fifteen: "connection" (*conexão*) is the word that moves cases from one building to another. When you see it in a ruling, ask who invoked it and what they gain from the change of address.
