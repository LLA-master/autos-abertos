Of the 85 documents in Inquiry 5.035, 26 are petitions, 16 are powers of attorney and 23 are filing receipts. It is a proceeding in which the lawyers write more than the police and the judge put together. This last chapter is about them: who asked to be let in, when, and what that says about the reach the parties attribute to an inquiry about influencers.

## Before it existed

The first to knock at the door was Thiago Miranda, owner of Miranda Comunicação, the Agência MiThi. On 29 January 2026, one day after the opening order and eleven days before the inquiry was docketed at the Supreme Court, his lawyers filed with the Federal Police's Directorate for the Investigation and Combat of Organised Crime a request for access, "considering the recent newspaper reports about the possible opening of a police inquiry involving the Petitioner's name". The power of attorney had been signed on 10 January, the day after the G1 report that had named him. No other character in the case moved so far ahead.

On 6 March, he was served notice to give a statement on the 12th. On 9 March, his lawyers went to the justice: "this defence has no knowledge of the content of the record", "the mere formal granting of access is not enough", and, "should the appointed date be kept without the record first being made available", the client "will reserve his constitutional right to silence". On 31 March, the reporting justice granted access, "temporary", "subject to the restrictions inherent to the sealed nature". The interview of 12 March does not appear as having taken place in the filings this site has read.

On 23 April, the police chief served notice on him again, "considering the latest news carried by the mainstream media and the authorisation of the Supreme Federal Court", for 29 April, in person in Brasília or by video conference. The day before, a lawyer sent a medical certificate by e-mail: the client had undergone surgery on 28 March, with complications and hospitalisation, and had "absolute rest" recommended for 45 days; it would be "reckless" to hear him before 12 May. The police chief rescheduled. On 12 July, a new lawyer for the businessman complained that he "only has access to the filings added up to 7 April 2026". What happened between April and July, the record does not show, because the police added nothing after 4 May.

## The journalist

The second person served notice was Leonardo Antônio Lima Dias, Léo Dias, also for 29 April. The notice was sent to two e-mail addresses; the defence said it had learned of it "only today, 28.04.2026", through "one of his old personal e-mail addresses", and asked for access to the file, a postponement "sine die" and for the interview to take place in São Paulo, where he lives "on account of professional commitments, such as television programmes", or by video conference. It attached, as proof of residence, a lease in the name of his company. The exchange of e-mails between the law firm and the police registry is in the record and is the portrait of an inquiry run as a service counter: the lawyer explains that she goes on holiday on 14 May, that she needs "to buy tickets and book a hotel", that she is available on 13 May; the police clerk informs her that the São Paulo regional headquarters can receive the deponent and that "the access granted by the Federal Police does not replace that obtained directly from the Supreme Court". The new notice went out for 13 May, in Brasília, "with the possibility of attending the Regional Headquarters in São Paulo". There is no record of a statement in the filings. On 30 July, the lawyers asked for access to the filings added "after 11.05.2026, the date of the last access".

What the record says about why the journalist was served notice is what the January report had said: "according to tax-authority data, there is a relationship between Thiago Miranda and the communicator Léo Dias", because the agency's partner "is also the administrator of the company Léo Dias Comunicação e Jornalismo S.A.", and "the same telephone number appears registered" under both company registration numbers. The journalist, in his petition, said that he "has every interest in cooperating with the investigation", that he "has no knowledge of the reasons that justified the scheduling of his interview, nor even of the object of investigation in this record", and that he wants to be heard "as soon as possible". No filing calls him a suspect.

## The queue

The rest is the list of those who asked for admission of counsel, with dates, and the site reproduces it because the list is, in itself, the information. Daniel Vorcaro, on 4 March, through three lawyers; on 13 and 17 March, through transfers of the brief; on 27 April, through a new power of attorney, granting powers to act in "Inq 5026, Pet 15198, Rcl 88121, Inq 5035, Pet 15556, Pet 15562, Pet 15612, Pet 15504, Pet 15499, Pet 15711, Pet 15712, Pet 15771, Pet 15772", a list that is the map of the case as seen by the defence; on 22 May and 25 August, further transfers of the brief. Marilson Roseno da Silva, the "supervisor" of the series on Petition 15.556, on 17 March. Henrique Moura Vorcaro, on 11 May, with a power of attorney for this inquiry and for four other petitions. Belline Santana, the Central Bank department head detained in March, on 18 May, saying he had found, in the police requests in Petitions 15.556 and 15.562, "the reference to the present INQ 5035", and asking for access "to the full record", "not merely the provision of a 'list of filings with individual access'". Paulo Henrique Bezerra Rodrigues Costa, the former president of BRB, on 29 July.

And, on 29 May, Senator Ciro Nogueira. The petition is two pages long and says that he "was surprised, on 7 May 2026, by the execution of a search and seizure warrant at his residence, issued by order of this Honourable Supreme Federal Court, in the context of the so-called Operation Compliance Zero", that he "is at the absolute disposal of all the authorities", and that he asks for admission "in the present record and in all other related proceedings and incidents". It is the only mention, in the entire public archive this site has read so far, of a search at the home of a senator of the Republic in the Master case. The influencers' inquiry does not say why the search took place, nor in which proceeding it was ordered; the senator asked for access to all of them, and this was one. Two weeks earlier, on 14 May, a businessman from Teresina with the surname Nogueira Lima had asked for admission in the same inquiry; on 15 June, a business administrator resident in Lisbon asked for access to this and eleven other proceedings in the case; on 17 August, one more. The site does not name the three because no filing says what links them to the facts.

## What the queue shows

The list has a logic that the inquiry does not explain but the archive suggests. The defence teams of the main suspects ask for access to everything because, as Belline Santana wrote, the police requests cite the proceedings within one another, "with the specific reference" to 5.035, and nobody wants to be caught out by an element that sat in a record they had not read. An inquiry about posts from December 2025 thus becomes a mandatory item for anyone defending against detention, asset freezes and accusations of corruption at the Central Bank. Not because it holds evidence against them, but because it might, and Binding Precedent 14 gives whoever is under investigation the right to check.

On the police side, the tally is thin and the site records it without adjectives. Two witnesses heard in February; transcripts made on 27 April, two and a half months later; two persons served notice who, up to the last filing, have no record of a statement in the file; a request for an extension in March, granted, and the dispatch of "the steps carried out to date" to the Supreme Court on 4 May. After 4 May, between 20 July and 25 August, there are only petitions. The 60-day deadline granted on 31 March expired at the end of May, and there is no new extension in the record. It is possible that one exists and was not published; it is possible that the inquiry was absorbed by another; it is possible that it has stalled. The archive does not allow one to choose.

What it does allow one to say is this. The December campaign existed, and the posts vanished. Two people who declined it documented the refusal before a notary. A company put "Project DV" in a contract. An agency paid at least one influencer, according to the press. Between those facts and the sentence in the opening order, "the suspect Daniel Bueno Vorcaro had allegedly hired a marketing company", there is a distance that the inquiry, in seven months, has not covered in the public record. This site does not know whether the distance is impassable or whether simply nobody has crossed it yet. It knows that, in September 2026, the only document in the proceeding linking the banker's name to the campaign is the same one as in January: a two-letter acronym, explained by the person who read it.

> Reading rule number twenty-four: count the powers of attorney. When they outnumber the evidence, the proceeding has stopped being a place where one investigates and has become a place where one watches what the others are investigating.
