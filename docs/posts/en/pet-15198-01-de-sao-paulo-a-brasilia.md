Petition 15.198 begins with a single sheet of paper. Just one. On 22 December 2025, the Prosecutor General, Paulo Gonet, asks Justice Dias Toffoli for "the opening of an autonomous proceeding, in a separate docket, classified at the highest secrecy grade provided for (level 4)", to be assigned "by prior connection (*por prevenção*) to Constitutional Complaint No. 88.121". No name, no fact, no request on the merits. It is a drawer being opened. What went into it over the following weeks is the largest proceeding in the public archive: 1,041 filings, 122,857 pages, almost two thirds of everything the STF has published on the case. This series tells how.

The usual warning applies: everything here is investigation stage. Nobody has been indicted in this record. What the police say is what the police say; what the judge decides is whether the police may carry on.

## The PDF

The real story begins earlier, and the ruling of 6 January 2026 reconstructs it by quoting the federal prosecutors in São Paulo. The Federal Police "received an anonymous tip at its institutional e-mail address, containing a file in PDF format entitled 'INFORMAÇÕES BANCO MÁSTER'". Spelt like that, with an accent on Máster, as it appears in the record. A preliminary investigation was opened; the steps taken "corroborated the content" and it became an inquiry before the 8th Federal Criminal Court of São Paulo, under way "since 27 May 2025". An anonymous tip by e-mail is the formal origin of the proceeding that today runs to 122,000 pages. It is the kind of detail the press leaves out because it looks banal, and the record puts in because it is the law.

The São Paulo inquiry produced a series of police intelligence reports (*Informações de Polícia Judiciária*), each with a protocol number, each digging on one side: the financial statements of Banco Máxima and later of Master, from 2019 to 2024; the bank's investments; the financial-intelligence reports; the sanctions by the securities commission (CVM) on "at least 5 other occasions". What the police say they found is left for chapter 2, because it is the financial part and deserves room. What matters here is the route.

## The São Paulo request

On that basis, the Federal Police applied to the São Paulo judge for four things, which Toffoli's ruling lists on its first page: the pre-trial detention of Daniel Vorcaro; search and seizure "against thirty-eight targets"; the sequestration and freezing of assets "up to the limit of R$ 5,775,234,097.25"; and the lifting of bank and tax secrecy "of one hundred and one persons and entities", for the period from 20 October 2020 to 21 October 2025, "the period in which the suspects were already in control of BANCO MÁSTER".

On 27 October 2025, the São Paulo prosecutors opined for partial approval. On 24 November, the federal judge asked for clarifications: he wanted to know, among other things, whether this did not overlap with other investigations, "Fundo Fake" and "Compliance Zero", and what the Central Bank's part would be. On 27 November, the Federal Police replied, itemised the amounts to be frozen and added a target, João Carlos Falbo Mansur, "on the basis of a communication made by the Central Bank of Brazil to the federal prosecution service, bringing indications of crimes against the national financial system (SFN) involving Banco Master and REAG TRUST DTVM". On 28 November, the São Paulo prosecutors took the request on board and argued, citing a precedent of the STJ (the Superior Court of Justice), that jurisdiction lay with the federal courts in São Paulo, "given that the facts under investigation occurred in this judicial subsection", on Rua Brigadeiro Faria Lima, "a public and notorious fact".

The São Paulo thesis that there was no overlap is a small treatise on the borders between inquiries, and it is worth reading. Compliance Zero, said the São Paulo MPF, "has as its object the investigation of the crime of fraudulent/reckless management of BANCO BRB, with the participation of some persons linked to BANCO MÁSTER"; the São Paulo inquiry investigates "the management of BANCO MÁSTER, without involving BANCO BRB". And "there are only 3 targets in common" between the two, "according to what has been reported in the media". The earlier inquiries into funds were aimed at "administrators, managers and custodians", not at the bank's managers. Conclusion: "it is evident that there is no overlap". Hold on to that sentence; it is going to lose.

## The complaint that changed everything

On 5 December, the São Paulo judge suspended the proceedings. The reason lay in Brasília: two days earlier, on 3 December, Justice Toffoli, reporting justice of Constitutional Complaint 88.121, had ordered the record of Operation Compliance Zero "and connected cases" sent to the STF, and had written a sentence the January ruling transcribes: "in the face of an investigation supposedly directed against persons holding a privileged venue by virtue of office, as indeed already reported by the formal media, the jurisdiction of the constitutional court is fixed". Any measure, "including in other connected investigations", would have to pass "beforehand" through the Supreme Court, "and no longer through the lower court".

The mechanics are these: when there is an indication that someone with privileged venue at the STF may be involved, the whole investigation moves up, at least until "the actual participation of the authority holding privileged venue" is known. The ruling does not say who the authority is, and this text does not guess. What is known is the effect: on 23 December, the São Paulo prosecutors asked for the transfer; the same day, Gonet opened the drawer in Brasília. The São Paulo MPF's sentence about there being no overlap, written on 28 November, lasted a week.

## What the PGR asked for, and what it did not

The PGR's submission in Brasília, which the ruling reproduces in its opening pages, "partially endorses" the São Paulo request. It backs the lifting of secrecy, the freezes with the corrected amounts and the search against Mansur. But it withdraws one item: Vorcaro's detention. The argument is one of procedural economy, not of merits. Vorcaro was already under alternative measures imposed by a habeas corpus at the TRF1 (the federal appeals court for the 1st region), the same court that had revoked the November detention: periodic reporting, a ban on contact with other suspects, retention of his passport, "suspension of the exercise of any activity of an economic or financial nature" and an ankle monitor. "There is no report of non-compliance", the PGR writes, "the suspect's situation being settled." That was January. In March, the same PGR would ask for more time to consider the detention that another reporting justice would order in Petition 15.556, and the reader of the other series knows how that ended.

## The ruling of 6 January

Toffoli ruled in twenty pages, fifteen of them transcription. It is a ruling of passage: it accepts the transfer "to prevent future uncertainty about the legitimacy of acts performed" and "to avoid nullities"; it acknowledges that "the reporting justice's assessment, at this stage, is necessarily limited"; and it grants, "in the exact terms in which they were formulated", the lifting of bank and tax secrecy and the freezes. The formula he uses for the crimes is the PGR's, almost word for word: a criminal organisation "devoted to the fraudulent management of a financial institution, inducing or keeping investors in error, insider trading and market manipulation, and money laundering". And the formula for the method also comes from the police: "systematic exploitation of vulnerabilities in the capital market and in the regulatory and supervisory system", "through the use of investment funds and an intricate network of entities connected to one another by corporate, family or professional ties".

An institutional detail that the ruling records in passing, and that changes the tone of everything: the São Paulo inquiry was, in Toffoli's words, "even broader than those [investigations] that are the object of 'Operation Compliance', under my charge". The operation that gives the public case its name began in the Federal District, with BRB; the investigation that measures the bank from the inside began in São Paulo, with a PDF. At the Supreme Court, the two met in a drawer with level-4 secrecy.

Two notes to close. First: this ruling detained nobody and ordered no searches; the searches came two days later, in a separate ruling, and are the subject of chapter 3. Second: the reporting justice who signs "Ministro DIAS TOFFOLI" in January is not the one who signs this petition's rulings from February on. The record shows, on 13 February, a three-word order by the president of the Court, Edson Fachin, directing redistribution. It does not say why. Chapter 4 deals with what came with the new reporting justice.

> Reading rule number seven: in a court case, the "where" decides the "who". Before asking what a judge decided, ask why it was that judge who was deciding.
