Section 2.2.6 of the request has a long title: "on the compromise of the secrecy of the 1st phase of Operation Compliance Zero, with Daniel Vorcaro's consequent action to monitor the authorities investigating him, obstruct the course of justice and evade criminal prosecution". It is the most delicate part of the filing, because in it the police do not accuse only the banker. They accuse, without saying who, someone on the inside. And it is the part that speaks to a ruling by another court, which released Vorcaro ten days after his arrest on the strength of a document that, according to this petition, was written by an official who worked for him.

## The notes

On 17 October 2025, at around 9 am, there was "a closed meeting between the Federal Police and members of the BCB to deal with the ongoing investigation involving Banco Master". Four days later, on 21 October, Vorcaro recorded in the "Notes" app on his phone the names of the Federal Police representatives who had attended that meeting: the director of investigation and organised-crime enforcement, the head of the financial-crimes division, an officer "who is one of the signatories of this report", one police chief and another police chief who took part remotely. Also listed, "although they were not at the meeting", were the names of three federal prosecutors.

Minutes later, at 1:38 pm, another note, entitled "From people inside the Central Bank (*Bacen*) who are my friends and got worried". In the body, according to the report of 19 February, he "stated that those people had taken part in the meeting, which is why the information about the names and the measures mentioned was, according to him, 100% reliable". And that he could not "burn them".

On 16 November, two days before the operation, a third note, which the police read as the draft of a message, because it contains the question "Are you two close?". In it, the banker asks someone, not identified, whether that person "was close to the federal judge Ricardo Soares Leite, the same one who ordered the arrest". The request underlines: "since this was a sealed inquiry, at that point (16/11/2025) there would be no known reason or lawful means for the target of the investigation to know the name and jurisdiction of the federal judge in charge of the case".

Who the friends were, the police do not say. Vorcaro, questioned in August 2026 in Petition 15.504, said he "had people who kept him informed" and that he "does not recall who".

## The morning

The 17th begins early. At 6:34 am, Vorcaro is already talking to Paulo Sérgio and Belline, the two heads of supervision at the Central Bank. At 7:28:32 am, he receives messages from the lawyer, with whom, according to the phone, he had not exchanged messages since 3 August, "to deal with an 'important' matter". The content, the police do not have: "view-once photos and voice notes" and calls. The effect, they do: shortly afterwards, Vorcaro writes "don't schedule the meeting any more because now I've changed the plan" (*mudei o roteiro*) and, at 8:48 am, says he is "booking a flight 'leaving from Congonhas'".

At 7:41 am, he opens a conversation with the journalist Diego Escosteguy. "After coordination on the content of the article to be published in the banker's interest", the filing says, the journalist sends the link shortly after 11 am. The text, reproduced in the record, spoke of "adversaries of both parties" mobilised to "blow up both banks" and reported that "one of the fronts of attack is at the 10th Federal Court of Brasília", where "the inquiry that resulted from the Central Bank's opinion is pending". Vorcaro passes the link "immediately" to the lawyer. And the lawyer shows him a petition, filed at the 10th Court that same day, requesting access to the investigation and citing, "in at least one of its versions, the piece published by Diego Escosteguy as the source of the information". Over the course of the day, the lawyer says he is "hounding" (*infernizando*) the judge, "including through direct WhatsApp messages addressed to the magistrate", and sends him a news report about the Fictor group's interest in buying Master, a story which, "not by coincidence", appeared in the press "for the first time, on the same day". There are exchanges, the police say, "indicating that Daniel Vorcaro was responsible" for the leak. The report of 19 February is more specific: the story was passed by him, directly, to the editor-in-chief of a Brasília news site.

The lawyer is not under investigation in this record and is not named here. The police's conclusion about him is a disjunction: Vorcaro "had prior knowledge of the coming police operation, through unlawful means, whether through Central Bank officials or through contact from" the lawyer.

## The afternoon

In the WhatsApp group with the two officials, Paulo Sérgio and Belline conclude, "after a few voice calls", that it is "essential" for Vorcaro to speak to the director of supervision, Ailton de Aquino. Vorcaro tries; the director does not answer and proposes a slot for the next day, the 18th; the banker "insists on talking the same day". He proposes 1 pm; Paulo Sérgio confirms 1:15 pm; Belline sends the link at 1:35 pm. The meeting, recorded in the director's public diary, runs from 1:30 pm to 2:10 pm. Hours later, Paulo Sérgio sends the group the news report on the sale to Fictor. "There is no reaction of surprise", the police note; Paulo Sérgio says he liked it, Vorcaro replies that he "could not prepare it the way he wanted".

That night, the Federal Police "intercepted the banker" at Guarulhos airport, "about to board a private jet with final destination in the United Arab Emirates". The next morning, the operation.

## The same meeting, seen from the TRF1

Here the petition annexes two documents from elsewhere. The first is the ruling of 28 November 2025 by appellate judge Solange Salgado, of the TRF1 (the federal appeals court for the 1st region), which reconsidered the interim order and replaced pre-trial detention with alternative measures, extended to Augusto Lima, Luiz Antonio Bull, Alberto Felix and Angelo Antonio Ribeiro da Silva. The defence had submitted three requests for reconsideration; with the third, it attached a "document proving that the petitioner, in a video-conference meeting with the Central Bank on the morning of 17/11/2025, expressly communicated that he would travel to Dubai that same day to sign the sale of the bank". The appellate judge accepted it: the risk of flight "appears controllable", because he "gave the Central Bank prior notice of his international trip", "during an official meeting held on the same date as the departure".

The second document is that very document. It is "Informações e Despacho 36790/2025", from Desup, dated 25 November 2025, signed by Paulo Sérgio Neves de Souza as deputy head. It answers a request from Vorcaro, through his attorney, of 24 November. The answers: "a) the video conference was not recorded; b) the matters discussed were not recorded in minutes, notes or any other medium; and c) the Director of Supervision and Desup received no correspondence, e-mail or written message from the petitioner" about Fictor or the trip. And then, the account of what was said: that the banker was negotiating with Mastercard the release of guarantees, that he was working to sell the group "in three parts", that he had asked for the hearing scheduled for the 19th to be brought forward because he intended to announce the sale to a domestic group by the end of the 17th and "would travel to Dubai, in the United Arab Emirates, that same day, for the signing of the contract".

The police's argument is short and hard. "The meeting was not recorded, nor was its content set down in minutes. The only reference to the matters discussed is the statement signed by Paulo Sérgio, who, as shown, acts as a veritable consultant to Master." And: "even if the content of the statement is true", Vorcaro "took the initiative and insisted on the meeting, subsequently scheduled with the support of Paulo Sérgio and Belline, only after learning that he was an imminent target". In other words: the document that convinced the TRF1 there was no flight was produced, according to the police, by the official who, in the police accusation, was being paid by the banker, about a meeting the banker himself forced that morning.

Paulo Sérgio, questioned in July 2026 in 15.504, said he "honestly has no such recollection" of having pressed the director. Vorcaro said he "told the Central Bank in the morning that he had a solution". The document says what it says. Whoever reads it decides what it proves.

## The whiteboard

There remains the last annex, the report on the search at the bank's headquarters on 18 November. The team arrived at 6 am and, according to the police chief who signs it, security "initially tried to prevent our access to the building, claiming they had no facial-recognition authorisation"; it opened up after being warned that entry "would be carried out by use of force". Thirteen floors, "around 500 employees", a main office "completely empty" and the other, Vorcaro's, used as a base. In it, "a sketch of deals which, according to the company's lawyer, had been worked on the previous night", with "a mention of TITAN CAYMAN, apparently a trust in the Cayman Islands". On the seventh floor, two safes, one with documents and the other with "a small amount of cash, including foreign currency". At 7 am, "an IT employee working with the lights off", interviewed; the forensic experts "identified no relevant elements". On the tenth floor, the legal department, "no seizure was carried out", on instructions from the coordination unit.

The request makes the whiteboard the last piece of the 17th: "on the same night he intended to travel abroad, after learning of the inquiry and seeking access to its content, the banker worked on a sketch of what is an evident plan of financial and corporate flows, with 'TITAN CAYMAN' as its central figure". What Titan was, if it exists, and what the sketch said beyond arrows and acronyms, the record does not tell. The board was photographed; the photo is illegible in the public copy.

> Reading rule number thirty-seven: a leak is proved by what the victim of the leak knew ahead of time. The police have three dated notes and the name of a judge that nobody should have known. They do not have the source. Until they do, "friends inside the Bacen" is a sentence from the suspect, not evidence against anyone. And the document that freed him is still worth what a piece of paper is worth with no recording, no minutes and a signature under suspicion.
