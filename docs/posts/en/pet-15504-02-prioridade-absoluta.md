The central document of Petition 15.504 is neither a ruling nor a statement. It is a Police Intelligence Report on Material (*Informação de Polícia Judiciária de Material*), IPJ-M 144738439.2026, signed on 18 February by five federal police officers, on the contents of an iPhone 17 Pro seized from Daniel Vorcaro when he was arrested, in November 2025. It runs to 131 pages, 133 figures, nine tables. It is the police hypothesis with illustrations, and everything that follows should be read as such: as what the police chose to show, in the order they chose to show it.

## Two notes

The document begins with two entries in the phone's Notes app. On 17 October 2025, the police met in secret with the Central Bank to discuss the Master investigation. Four days later, at 7:08 pm on 21 October, Vorcaro recorded on his phone the names of five police chiefs and officers present at that meeting, with their respective posts. On 30 October, he wrote that he would travel to Abu Dhabi, described the bank's situation and noted having received "confidential information from members of the BCB" that "G" and the directors were "once again under strong pressure" from the Federal Police and the prosecution service. Twenty-five minutes later, another note, headed "From people inside the BCB who are my friends and got worried": the people "took part in the meeting", so the information was "100% reliable", and he "could not burn them".

The police do not say who the friends were. They record the notes, record that the names match those of the 17 October meeting, and move on to the dialogues. The reader should do the same: the notes prove that the banker knew; they do not prove who told him.

## The deputy

The conversation with Paulo Sérgio Neves de Souza begins, on the phone, on 20 October 2023, when the official sends Vorcaro the order appointing him deputy head of Desup. The police read the gesture as "interest, on the banker's part, in knowing of his new position". From then on, in the IPJ's chronology, the deputy gives guidance.

In December 2023, Vorcaro wants to present "the final design" of the purchase of a payment institution and the plan for an investment bank; Paulo Sérgio replies that the matter is "absolute priority". On 21 December, the official "takes the liberty" of suggesting the topics Vorcaro should take to the meeting with the "Presi", whom the police infer to be Roberto Campos Neto: strategy, governance, court-ordered government debt (*precatórios*) bought "with the prior consent of supervision", the history of "investigations" by the FGC. On 5 February 2024, new arguments for another meeting, this time with the director Ailton, about Banif, Voiter and Will. On 5 April, Paulo Sérgio passes on a message of thanks from a third party: the Voiter negotiation had been concluded "in record time" thanks to his efforts. The IPJ underlines: "there would have been 'effort' by the BCB official to bring a private deal to completion".

There is more. On 29 December 2023, the deputy says he "has" a buyer for Letsbank; on 25 October 2024, he reports that Nubank is interested, asks about figures and says he will pass them on. In May 2024, Vorcaro asks for "the BCB's support" against a rule change by Previc that would make it harder to sell financial bills (*letras financeiras*) to pension funds. On 7 November 2024, he sends a file called "Compromisso BCB. Vfinal" and asks whether it is "ok"; Paulo Sérgio suggests adding that long-term operations will cease. The police note the detail that sets the tone of the petition: "one of the final addressees of the document is PAULO SÉRGIO himself". On 25 April 2025, the same: Vorcaro sends the draft of a letter to Desup answering a summons (*termo de comparecimento*) about liquidity, and the deputy head of Desup sends it back with changes.

On 17 February 2025, the message the police treat as a leak: "there was an outflow of R$ 488 million from the Reag Growth fund and an inflow of R$ 545 million from Master Holding Financeira", a movement that "set off the filter" (*caiu no filtro*) of the monitoring unit. And BRB: on 2 January 2025, almost three months before the public announcement, Vorcaro is already sending a draft agreement between the Federal District, BRB, the pension institute of the Federal District's civil servants and an "investor"; in March, the deputy asks whether the premium on the sale of portfolios to BRB "would be R$ 5 billion"; Vorcaro celebrates "approved by BRB's board" and "Teter is in", a funding round of about R$ 1 billion; and asks whether it would be possible to "pull" the supervision of BRB from Belo Horizonte to São Paulo, "so that Ailton could 'do it'".

In the FGC chapter, the police show Master's letter to the fund before and after a suggestion by Paulo Sérgio on paragraph 13: where it read that there was "a coordinated movement by institutions to obstruct the free operation and distribution of CDBs", it came to read "a movement to obstruct the free operation and the distribution of CDBs". And they close with a conversation between Vorcaro and his brother-in-law, Fabiano Zettel, who is going to meet the official that day and asks for guidance. The answer: treat him well, because he is being "an angel in the life" of the banker.

## The chief

With Belline Santana, head of Desup, the material is smaller and of a different nature. On 24 November 2023, Belline asks to speak to Vorcaro about the CVM, a body that was not his own; the police record that "it is plausible to infer" that the information reached him through his post. On 9 March 2025, he warns that "the M&A information" had not yet been confirmed. On 11 April 2024, he sets up a three-way meeting on the theme "prospective and strategic vision". On 1 March 2025, seeing the bank's "schedule of inflows", he writes: "We need to get to the other side". The IPJ reads the first person plural as a sign that the two "would have coinciding interests"; in August, it is Vorcaro who writes "We are going to win this war".

In April 2025, Belline asks for a draft, receives in the small hours the document on the corporate reorganisation between BRB and Master, addressed to the Central Bank's director of Organisation, and sends back "feedback", saying that he "will talk internally to assess the feasibility". On 1 October, he gives his view on a Master letter to the FGC about the sale of Will: "it came out very well", no suggestions. On 14 March 2024, a message indicates that he is at the gate of Vorcaro's house, asking for the number.

## The group

On 2 October 2025, Belline created a WhatsApp group with Vorcaro and Paulo Sérgio. The police call it the "Master" group. The context, in the IPJ, is that of a bank that had already lost the merger with BRB and was negotiating a credit line from the FGC and the sale of Will. Over the next seventeen days, the IPJ shows: the FGC's letter refusing the bank a new assistance line, shared in the group, followed by the draft reply "ratified" by Belline; Vorcaro's question, on 4 October, whether "there was any conversation with Ailton"; a meeting set with the director for 24 October; a draft letter to Desup, the department of the two men, on the bank's "solution plan"; the query about "releasing" a letter to BTG "along the same lines" as the one given to an insurer, and Paulo Sérgio's reply that Ailton "would be up for sending the letter along the insurer's lines, since that would be the wording the PGBC would approve"; Vorcaro's question whether it was not possible to "put the squeeze on Cristiano" (*dar um aperto*); Belline's celebration when "Deorf said it approved the capital"; the request that the Central Bank give Mastercard "a knock" (*dar uma batida*), as it was demanding additional guarantees from Will; and a new letter, on 3 November, asking for reconsideration of the suspension of portfolio purchases by BRB.

On 17 November 2025, the eve of the liquidation, the group wakes early. Paulo Sérgio writes that the week is decisive. Vorcaro speaks of a deal "already closed with the Mubadala fund", which would depend on the Central Bank "pressing" Mastercard, and suggests that "Belline and Ailton themselves" do it. After calls among the three, he asks whether he should call Ailton; the two say yes; Ailton does not answer. At 1:00 pm Vorcaro proposes a meeting; at 1:34 pm Ailton, in the private chat, sends a link titled "Market solution – Banco Master" (*Solução de mercado – Banco Master*); at 1:35 pm Belline sends the same link in the group. Hours later, the news report on the sale of Master to Fictor circulates in the group. Paulo Sérgio says he liked it; Vorcaro replies that he could not prepare it the way he wanted. And adds that he hopes "there was time for ailton to warn galipolo".

The police record that the meeting appears in the director's public diary, but that "the date on which it was scheduled does not appear". And they close the section with a screenshot sent to Vorcaro by one of his lawyers, that same night, showing messages exchanged with a contact the police infer to be the judge who would decide the next day's precautionary measures. The IPJ says this "indicates" that Vorcaro knew about the measures. It is the document's only inference about another authority, and it goes no further in this petition.

## The conclusions

The final eight pages organise everything along three axes. First, "improper influence": Paulo Sérgio "did not confine himself to passing on information, but actively intervened to adjust decisions, facilitate meetings, guide negotiations and shape official documents"; Belline "acted consistently and repeatedly as an informal consultant". Both, the police say, breached article 10 of the bank's staff Code of Conduct, which forbids consultancy, "paid or not", to anyone with dealings with the Central Bank. Second, "improper advantages": a trip to Disney for Paulo Sérgio and the sale of a farm; for Belline, a "flow of monthly and repeated payments" and a trip to Paris. Third, "money laundering": Zettel and Leonardo Palhares as "financial operators". That is the subject of the next chapter.

What the document does not have is as important as what it has. There is no bank statement: the IPJ asks for disclosure orders against Zettel, Palhares and 34 companies linked to the two, and the list of companies takes up two pages. There is no one's version: the 133 figures are clippings of conversation, and chosen clippings. And there is, in February 2026, no proof that any Central Bank decision was taken, or left untaken, because of these conversations. The police infer that from the words. The reader may infer the same, or not. But should know that he is inferring.

> Reading rule number twenty-nine: a Police Intelligence Report is a hypothesis with figures. Whoever chose the figures already had the hypothesis. None of that makes it false; it makes it a piece of accusation, and a piece of accusation is read with the mind of someone who has yet to hear the other side.
