Constitutional complaint (RCL) 88.121 is the smallest of the fifteen proceedings in the archive in number of pages of rulings and the largest in consequence. It was what took Operation Compliance Zero out of the 10th Federal Court of Brasília and put it in the hands of a single Supreme Court justice. It all began with a folder. This series tells, in five chapters, how a folder turned into a question of jurisdiction, what the Supreme Court did with it in twenty-five days, and what kept arriving in the case after it had been officially closed.

The usual warning: what follows is what the parties wrote and what the justices decided, at the precautionary stage, without any judgment on the merits about anyone. The congressman whose name appears in the record is not under investigation in it, and the Supreme Court's own ruling says it is not yet possible to state that the facts "bear a direct relation to the exercise of his mandate". This site repeats the caveat every time the name appears.

## What was seized

On 18 November 2025, the Federal Police executed search warrants at addresses of Daniel Vorcaro's, among them a house in Lago Sul, Brasília. The handwritten search record listed at item 4 a "folder belonging to Federal Congressman João Carlos Bacelar (PL-BA), with a document of an option agreement for the purchase of real estate". Three days later, the police filed the formal seizure record, and in it, according to the defence, "omitted the mention of the federal congressman's name", indicating only that the documentation belonged "generically to the 'Chamber of Deputies'".

That difference between the handwritten note and the form is the cornerstone of the complaint. Vorcaro's defence, signed by Roberto Podval, Pierpaolo Bottini and Sérgio Leonardo, among others, wrote to the Supreme Court on 27 November, under the stamp "URGENT! COMPLAINANT IN CUSTODY": "the name of the Federal Congressman to whom the document found relates was suppressed". Neither the prosecution service nor the Brasília judge "took a position on the filing of the said documentation belonging to a holder of privileged venue". And the petition was addressed, by choice, to Justice Nunes Marques, reporting justice of Petition 13.884, Operation Overclean, which investigates the diversion of parliamentary budget amendments in Bahia and which, according to the defence, relying on newspaper reports, would also mention the same congressman. "It is the same parliamentarian identified in the search and seizure against the Complainant, and a similar instrument to the one that led to the transfer of the record in that investigation to the STF."

The legal argument is familiar to those who follow the court: the Supreme Court's jurisdiction to try members of Congress "extends to the supervision of criminal investigations", and, when an indication arises that one of them may be involved, the record has to go up. The defence cited two precedents that would return in the rulings to come. One, from October 2025, by Justice Flávio Dino, ordering the STJ to send the Supreme Court a record in which seized material mentioned a congressman. The other, from 2018, by Dias Toffoli himself, saying that the screening at first instance of seized material that mentions a parliamentarian "implies, indirectly, an unequivocal and prohibited investigation of a holder of privileged venue". The complaint landed, by lot or by prior assignment (*prevenção*), with Toffoli.

## What else the petition asked for

It would be naive to read the complaint only as a jurisdictional thesis. Half of its 27 pages are about the detention. The defence argued that the Central Bank's liquidation of Master, ordered on the 18th, had emptied the risks that justified pre-trial detention: the liquidator "changes all the system passwords", Vorcaro "no longer holds the position of President or Director at the Bank or at any other regulated institution", "all the Complainant's corporate ties have been severed and his assets and funds have been frozen". It argued that "there is no fraud of 12 billion reais", because "of the R$ 12.76 billion reported by the press, more than R$ 10 billion have already been settled or replaced", citing BRB itself and a Central Bank letter. It argued that KPMG had certified the 2024 accounts, that "there has never been a sanctioning proceeding opened by the BC against Daniel Vorcaro", and that the trip cut short by the arrest was to "sign the contract for the sale of Banco Master to Fictor Holding and investors from the United Arab Emirates", with a hotel booked in Dubai "until 22 November" and a stop in Malta "only" because "the aircraft does not have the range to fly from Guarulhos to Dubai". The mention by the TRF1 (the federal appeals court for the 1st region) of a flight risk was called a "manoeuvre" that violates the ban on worsening the position of the party who appeals.

The final request was twofold: suspend the investigations in Brasília and send everything to the Supreme Court; and, "at a second stage", have the Supreme Court assess "the need to keep the complainant in detention".

## The value of the claim

The justice's first response, on 28 November, is one of the most curious filings in the archive. In five pages, Toffoli summarised the petition and then wrote: "It is noted that the initial petition does not state the value of the claim. This is a direct breach of articles 291 and 319, V, of the Code of Civil Procedure." An urgent constitutional complaint, from a man in custody, about usurpation of the Supreme Court's jurisdiction, stopped at the door for want of a number in the footer. The justice cited a judgment he himself had reported, saying that "the absence of economic content in the request made in the action does not exempt the party from the burden of assigning a value to the claim", gave fifteen days for amendment "on pain of dismissal", ordered information to be requested from the Brasília judge and sent the file to the PGR for comment. "I refrain from analysing, for now, the request for an interim order." Readers who think bureaucracy is the enemy of justice will find an argument here; those who think it a safeguard against haste, another.

On that same 28th, at the TRF1, appellate judge Solange Salgado was replacing Vorcaro's detention with alternative measures, and extending the ruling to the other detainees. The complaint, born to get a man out of jail, found him free before it was read.

## The third of December

The second response came on 3 December, and there the tone changes. Luiz Antonio Bull, a director of Master, had asked to be admitted to the case, saying he had been "incarcerated for 11 (eleven) days by reason of a decision issued by a judge who lacks jurisdiction to perform the coercive act", and warning that "hearings in the Inquiry are being scheduled, the material seized during the searches already analysed". The Federal Police had asked for access for its officers and had filed the said option agreement. Toffoli granted both things, and added the paragraph that changed the case's address: "until further consideration of the request, which is pending an opinion from the Prosecutor General's Office, new steps and measures must be submitted beforehand to the scrutiny of this Supreme Court, whose original jurisdiction is established, until a final decision on the present Complaint. Including as to other related investigations." And he explained: "in the face of an investigation supposedly directed at persons holding privileged venue, as indeed already reported by the mainstream media, the jurisdiction of the constitutional court is fixed."

The reader of the series on Inquiry 5.026 knows what happened that night in Brasília: a duty judge read that order and sent everything to the Supreme Court, and the prosecution service called it procedural chaos (*tumulto*). From the point of view of this complaint, what matters is what the justice had in his hands when he wrote it: a petition with no value of the claim, a request for admission to the case, a seizure record and newspaper reports. The PGR had not yet been heard. The Brasília judge had not yet replied.

## Those who came after

In the following days the complaint became the counter to which the suspects turned. On 5 December, Vorcaro reiterated his request for revocation of the alternative measures, arguing that the Supreme Court's "assumption of jurisdiction" made it "unnecessary to justify renewing the plea so shortly after the decision issued by the TRF". Alberto Felix de Oliveira Neto, the fifth man detained in November, filed a fifteen-page petition that is the fullest version of his defence in the whole archive: he "never sat on the board of Banco Master"; his last position was "Treasury Superintendent II"; he signed the contracts with Tirreno "as a mere agent", by power of attorney, "to make up the minimum quorum of two signatories"; he appears in none of the CVM proceedings on which the police request was based; the prosecution service asked for neither detention nor a freeze against him; and he was "dismissed for cause by Banco Master", with severance pay of "R$ 77,000", which, he argues, "would be utterly incompatible" with membership of a criminal organisation. He asked for the end of the ankle monitor, permission to work and the release of R$ 35,419.48 "to cover basic expenses". The reporting justice did not decide these requests at this stage; chapter 3 says what he did with them.

> Reading rule number seventeen: a constitutional complaint is a case about who decides, not about who did what. When the reading starts to look like a habeas corpus, it is because the parties know that better than the reader does.
