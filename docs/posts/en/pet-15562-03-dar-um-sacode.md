A warning before we begin. This chapter reproduces messages that the Federal Police attribute to Daniel Vorcaro, Felipe Mourão and Fabiano Zettel, extracted from the mobile phone seized in November 2025. They are the police's transcriptions, in the original spelling, translated here; none of the three has confirmed or denied them in this record. The people threatened are not under investigation and will not be named here, even where the record names them. And the police themselves write, in the report of 19 February, that "there is no proof, in the conversations examined, that the plans for physical assault were actually carried out". What there is, is the planning.

## The captain

The story begins on 28 May 2024, at 1:43 pm. Vorcaro sends Mourão a copy of the documents of a former employee, the captain of one of his boats, and "ordered that a full background check be run on him and that they would have to 'go after him'" (*ir pra cima*). Twenty minutes later, Mourão forwards a voice note from Marilson Roseno: "they were running the checks", but if "urgent support" was needed, they would be "standing by" (*em QAP*). The Turma wanted to know "how the incident took place and the reason for the alleged 'extortion'" and whether there was "a telephone number to be monitored". Vorcaro sends the number, explains that the man "had allegedly made a recording of him" and says: "we'll have to sit down with him". And adds: "he used to be a cop so he thinks he's a big shot". Mourão asks where Vorcaro is and says he "would send 'A turma' to Rio right then"; Vorcaro asks him to wait, "because he was still his employee".

At 2:09 pm the same day, a second target: the boat's chef, who "had supposedly gone along with it". The main objective, Vorcaro stresses, was the captain, but the checks were to cover both of them, "including their family members". The next day, Mourão: "the boys have everything on them for whatever's needed". On 1 June, the chef posts in his stories that he is going to "expose a situation related to his last job". Vorcaro sends the video: "we'll have to act before I'm back, ideally you go with Fabiano and with police. You have to go with two police officers". And a sentence the police underline: "I don't even know if a police crew or a numbers-game boss's (*bicheiro*) crew is better for a Rio lowlife like this. Police sometimes won't intimidate as much". Mourão: "You're the boss, tell me and I'll go with whoever you want". Then: "it'll be the PF then", and "if you want I'll go with the Rio people too, you just have to call". Vorcaro: "I think it has to be both", and "The good thing about giving the chef a shake (*dar sacode*) first is the other one will already get scared".

An hour later, Vorcaro writes to his brother-in-law. Zettel: "What an SOB! Sicário has the names and where to find them?". Vorcaro: "He has everything. Already checked it all out". And: "but we can't leave him on his own. You have to go to smooth things over" (*contemporizar*). It is this pair, the one who threatens and the one who smooths things over, that the police call "direct involvement in actions of physical pressure", in the summary table where Zettel appears as "brother-in-law of Daniel Bueno Vorcaro; took direct part in actions of physical pressure against victims".

## Seven militia members

What happened next, the police know from a recording of 25 minutes and 36 seconds that Vorcaro himself sent to Mourão on 11 July 2024, with the request: "I need you to delete it and not send it to anyone. I'm going to have trouble with this guy". And the comment: "it backfired. This guy was the main one. Should have met with him". Mourão: "But they did find him. The next day, it's a lie, he wasn't even threatened". The same day, two voice notes from Mourão: "the whip is in his hand"; "dirty tricks are paid back with dirty tricks"; the man was "just 'upset' at having lost his job"; "whatever he asks us to do, we go all in".

The recording, which the police believe to be "an ambient recording", is of a conversation between the captain and the owner of the company that managed the boat, on being told of his dismissal. The transcript is in the report of 26 February. The captain says: "Seven militia members, I have the footage from the boat, they were here, they went to my building. They threatened to kill me. Me and my family". He recounts that he was travelling, that his wife "calls in despair", that the doorman warned "that there were seven men here inside the Marina", and that, when asked, they said: "We are here on the orders of Mr Daniel". That they also went after the chef, at a hotel in Angra, "all of it filmed". That he has two telephone numbers of those who called. At 19 minutes and 37 seconds: "One of them even said he was a Federal Police officer from the SR here".

That is why the police asked to enter his home: the captain "stated that he keeps evidence that may be essential to identifying the other people involved in the 'Turma'". The justice granted it, noting that he "is not a suspect". The Ubatuba warrant was executed on 4 March; the Florianópolis one was not, because he no longer lived there. What was seized, and whether there was any footage, the record does not say.

## The housekeeper, the journalist, the DJ

The pattern, the police write, "was not an isolated incident". On 19 February 2025, Vorcaro sends Mourão someone's telephone number: "Another one for the Rio crew. Housekeeper [name omitted] threatening me. Can you believe it? That bitch has to be ground down" (*tem que moer*). On 8 June 2025, Mourão sends an article by the journalist Lauro Jardim: "This Lauro clocks in at 7 huh. Shall I put out a new one on you? Positive. Scumbag". Vorcaro: "Yes. Should have had people following this guy. To get everything on him". Mourão: "I'll do that". On 28 July: "This Lauro I want to have him beaten up. Break all his teeth. In a mugging". Mourão: "Can we? I'll look into it". Vorcaro: "Yes".

And there is the DJ. According to the report of 26 February, in a conversation about another matter, Vorcaro "said he wanted to hire someone to follow a person, who was said to be a DJ at parties", and "suggested staging an incident involving drugs". Mourão said he would align it with the Turma, "but stressed that the person hired should be someone from Miami". Vorcaro asked him to involve "the friend from Interpol" and said "that he would invest 10,000,000.00 to have that person taught a lesson and learn that nobody messes with his son". Mourão suggested hiring the DJ to play in Rio or Belo Horizonte; Vorcaro replied that in Rio "there would be pressure from the militia and the police, but suggested that pressure from Interpol would be more frightening". Hence, the police conclude, the forged "Interpol" letter of October 2024, seen in the previous chapter: "despite the initial wish to stage a red-handed arrest", the group "would have opted to intimidate by way of Interpol".

On what happened to the DJ, the filing says nothing. On the journalist, nothing either. The police are explicit only about the captain and the chef: "at least in relation to these individuals, the intimidation attempts went beyond the merely speculative".

## The ones who were bought

There is a less physical version of the same method, which is to pay. On 3 September 2024, Vorcaro sends an article from a website about a CVM proceeding against him and his father: "Man this thing with this daily is ridiculous. I don't get it. What's going on. I left this one in your hands". Mourão passes it on to the boys and explains that the outlet has several writers. Vorcaro: "It has to come down urgently. Take that blog down for good. I thought we had a partnership. And we had bought the guys". The same day, Mourão sends an image of the article offline: "He said he'll try to set up a filter in there, so they can't post any more headlines with banco master". In other messages, Mourão sends lists of links "that had supposedly already been removed" and says he has sorted out "negative links on Google to take them down and, at the same time, create positive content that shows up at the top of searches".

And the list of 11 November 2024, with "Sicário" and a reporter. The police state, on page 134, that "Daniel Vorcaro made cash payments to Diego Escosteguy in return for articles in Banco Master's interest", and cite "the regular payment of R$ 2,000,000.00". The assertion is the police's; the journalist is not under investigation in this record and was not heard in it. His article of 17 November 2025 reappears in the next chapter.

Finally, the continuation. The request gathers news reports from January 2026 about influencers approached by agencies to record videos "in which they were to defend Banco Master and defame the Central Bank", with confidentiality contracts and a penalty of R$ 800,000, in a "Projeto DV". A city councillor from Rio Grande do Sul, heard in Inquiry 5.035, said that at the meeting "it was crystal clear" that the client was Vorcaro. The police deduce that "even after his arrest, Daniel Vorcaro would go on using the digital environment", and call this "continued criminal activity in the virtual sphere". It is an inference: no message from the imprisoned banker to an agency appears in this filing.

## What the justice saw

The ruling of 3 March condenses it all into two lines: "serious threats, including physical ones, against people who went against Daniel Vorcaro's interests" and "the formation of a violent militia, under the command of Daniel Vorcaro, to satisfy his exclusively private interests". The word "militia" is the captain's, in the recording; the police adopted it; the justice repeated it. In the messages, the one who uses it is Vorcaro himself, when weighing whether to send "a police crew or a numbers-game boss's".

None of the three suspects has spoken about these conversations in this record. Zettel has been in detention since 4 March and asked for access to the file. Mourão was the target of a search and an arrest on the same morning. Vorcaro, questioned in August in Petition 15.504, said he "had people who kept him informed" and that he could not remember who. About the captain, the chef, the housekeeper, the journalist and the DJ, nobody said anything.

> Reading rule number thirty-six: the distance between "I want to have him beaten up" and a beating given is the whole difference between an ugly conversation and a crime. The police know this, which is why they wrote that they have no proof of execution. Keep that sentence. When someone says the record proves violence, ask which, against whom, and on what page the forensic medical examination is. As of September 2026, the only forensic medical examination in this record is the banker's, done on the morning he was arrested.
