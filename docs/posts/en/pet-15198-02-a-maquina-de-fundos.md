Every financial scheme described in a court file has a design. This petition's fits into one sentence from the Federal Police, transcribed by the prosecutors and reproduced by the justice: Banco Master "directed a considerable part of the funds raised from the market, through the issuance of CDBs, to investment funds, predominantly FIDCs, in which BANCO MÁSTER itself appeared as sole quotaholder", and those funds "were used to acquire commercial notes and receivables from companies that had corporate ties or personal relationships with the partners". The public's money comes in on one side as a bank deposit certificate; it goes out on the other as credit to companies of the house; in the middle, a fund with a single quotaholder, the bank itself. That is the accusation. This chapter explains the design and the figures the record uses to support it, with a caveat that matters more here than anywhere else: every number below comes from the police or the MPF, was presented in a request for precautionary measures, and has been through no defence, no final forensic examination and no trial.

## The acronyms

A FIDC is a Fundo de Investimento em Direitos Creditórios, a receivables fund: a fund that buys debts, that is, the right to collect from someone. A commercial note (*Nota Comercial*, NC) is a debt security a company issues to finance itself. A CRI is a Certificado de Recebíveis Imobiliários, debt backed by real estate. A CDB, the bank deposit certificate, is what the bank's customer buys thinking it is a savings account with interest. The gearing described in the record links the four: the bank sells CDBs, puts the money into FIDCs of which it is the sole quotaholder, and the FIDCs buy NCs and CRIs from companies linked to the bank's partners. If the companies do not pay, the fund loses; if the fund loses, the bank loses; if the bank fails, the deposit-guarantee fund (FGC) loses, that is, the system. The police call this "fraudulent management" and "systemic risk". The defence, when it speaks, will call it something else. It has not yet spoken in this record.

## The clinic

The example the São Paulo MPF chose to illustrate the design is Clínica Mais Médicos S.A. According to the transcribed police analysis, the company "issued R$ 361,147,355.00 in NCs without any guarantees, its paid-in share capital being zero and its gross annual operating revenue (R$ 54,079.64 in 2023) being exceeded by the amount of the debt more than 6,500 times over". The next sentence, also the MPF's, concerns the clinic's president and partner: despite presiding over "a company that raised millions", she "has no assets and was a beneficiary of emergency welfare payments in 2020 and 2021", which, for the prosecution, "shows that in reality she acted as a front" (*interposta pessoa*, "*laranja*"). She granted a power of attorney to Fernando Alves Vieira, whom the record links to "relatives of partners of BANCO MÁSTER".

The clinic would not be alone. The MPF cites Hospital da Criança São José, with R$ 372,476,329.00 in NCs, and says that "other companies linked to the partners, such as HOLDING AF S.A. and SIMETRIA PLANOS DE SAÚDE EIRELI, reproduced the same pattern". The sum that closes the reasoning: "of the more than R$ 3.5 billion invested by BANCO MÁSTER in funds of which it is sole quotaholder, approximately R$ 1.8 billion went to the acquisition of NCs issued by companies linked to the partners themselves". Consolidating "related transactions", one arrives at the figure that became the ceiling of the freeze: R$ 5,775,234,097.25.

## The funds, one by one

The most technical part of the ruling is the itemisation of the freezes fund by fund, which the Federal Police prepared at the São Paulo judge's request and the MPF endorsed. It works as a map of the network, and that is why this site uses it. For each fund there is an administrator, asset managers, assigning companies and people.

The FIDCs City and City 02 would share the same administrator, Sefer Investimentos DTVM, "currently SEFER Investimentos", formerly Foco DTVM, of Benjamim Botelho, and the asset managers WNT and Acura. The assignors: Benefício Intelectual, Simetria, Hospital da Criança São José, Confiance Life and Holding AF, linked, according to the police, to André Beraldo and Fernando Vieira, and through them to ABM Participações and ABM Investimentos, which would have "connections with HENRIQUE VORCARO, NATALIA VORCARO and FELIPE VORCARO" through two real-estate firms, Três Vale and Trancoso Eco. The freeze on City: R$ 302,586,000.00; on City 02: R$ 1,483,355,684.00, plus R$ 1,012,025,000.00 in CRIs of Base Securitizadora, attributed to César Ligeiro, Ricardo Xavier, Vicente Conte Neto, Antonio Conte and Vorcaro.

The FIDC MN I repeats "the same individuals", with R$ 48.2 million from Confiance, R$ 231.2 million from Simetria and R$ 179.9 million from Benefício Intelectual. The FIDC Alvarinho has as its main assignor Bello Pactum, of Flavio Aguetoni and Luis Fernando de Almeida, also partners in Banvox DTVM, "associated with MAURÍCIO QUADRADO, cited as one of the partners of BANCO MÁSTER": R$ 24,225,642.22. The FIDC Jeitto, administered by Master's own brokerage, would have as its assignor Jeitto Instituição de Pagamento, with "149.98% participation in the fund's net worth", around R$ 1,073,380,633.94. An assignor holding 150% of a fund is a sentence that only makes sense in creative accounting, and the police record it as it stands.

Two names appear with a special role. Ascendino Madureira Garcia, "alias 'DINO'", would be the "tactical operator and intermediary between DANIEL VORCARO and ARTUR FIGUEIREDO, responsible for the technical execution of the movements and accounting adjustments". And Nelson Tanure, "ultimate beneficiary of LORMONT PARTICIPAÇÕES", whose notes of R$ 73.7 million "made up 97% of the portfolio of the FIDC MARANTA", is "recorded as a hidden partner of BANCO MÁSTER" by the police, "for which reason the freezing of his assets should be in the same amount as that concerning DANIEL VORCARO". "Hidden partner" (*sócio oculto*) is an expression from the police request. In the record of this petition there is, as of publication, no answer from him.

## REAG

The second piece of the machine came from the Central Bank, by a request to the São Paulo MPF, and is the reason for the inclusion of João Carlos Falbo Mansur. The MPF transcribes a flowchart from the Central Bank that this text summarises. A company, Brain Realty, "came to owe R$ 459 million to BANCO MÁSTER", but "most of the amount borrowed was invested in paper of low liquidity and contingent realisation ('BESC notes')" (*Cártulas do BESC*), while two funds, Astralo 95 and Reag Growth 95, "became creditors of BANCO MÁSTER DE INVESTIMENTOS for a total of R$ 450 million in CDBs". Between April and May 2024, "a disbursement of R$ 1.45 billion by BANCO MÁSTER, with a return of R$ 1.38 billion through the acquisition of CDBs" by the two funds, which were then "gradually redeemed". One example with a date: on 21 May 2024, Astralo 95 redeemed "approximately R$ 800 million in CDBs, transferring R$ 650 million to the FIP TERMÓPILAS, which transferred that same amount to the company SUPER EMPREENDIMENTOS E PARTICIPAÇÕES LTDA., in an account held at BANCO MÁSTER".

The reader of the Pet 15.556 series knows Super Empreendimentos: it is the box from which, according to the other investigation, the payments to "A Turma" (the crew) and to the Central Bank officials went out. Here it appears at the other end, receiving R$ 650 million that left the bank and passed through a fund, a CDB and a FIP. If both descriptions are true, it is the same company in both halves of the case. The site does not assert that they are; it shows that the record links them.

The declared beneficiaries of the REAG funds would be three people surnamed Falbo Mansur, "who have family ties to the controller of REAG DTVM, JOÃO CARLOS FALBO MANSUR". But the Central Bank records "doubts as to the real controller": one fund in the chain, Galo Forte FIP, was 100% owned by Astralo 95 until November 2024 and, from December, became "DANIEL VORCARO (about 80%)", "in contradiction with public knowledge which, since the end of 2023, pointed to DANIEL VORCARO as the owner". In other words: the register said one thing, the market knew another, and the December change brought the register up to what the market knew.

## What the ruling did with all this

Little, and rightly so. Justice Toffoli judged no fund. He wrote that "the reporting justice's assessment, at this stage, is necessarily limited: what is examined, in short, is the coherence between the indications gathered in the investigations carried out and the indispensability of the measures requested". He found it coherent, granted the secrecy lifts and the freezes "in the exact terms in which they were formulated", and reserved "a review of the factual and evidentiary picture in the course of the investigations". Nothing more. In May and July 2026, when suspects asked for their property back, the new reporting justice would say that discussing the origin of assets is discussing "the merits themselves", and that the merits are not discussed in a precautionary proceeding. That is chapter 5.

Three questions remain for whoever reads the 122,000 pages, almost all of them annexes of this same machine: how much of the R$ 5.7 billion is money that existed and how much is a sum of transactions that repeat; who, among the 39 search targets, is a cog and who is a relative; and what the Central Bank knew in May 2024, when Astralo 95 was redeeming R$ 800 million in a day. The answer to the third lies, in part, in the other series.

> Reading rule number eight: "sole quotaholder" is the most important expression in this proceeding. A fund with one quotaholder is not a fund; it is a drawer with a fund's name on it.
