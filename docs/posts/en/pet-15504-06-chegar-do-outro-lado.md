In the earlier proceedings of this series, the suspects hardly speak: they stay silent or file petitions through counsel. In Petition 15.504, the three members of the group spoke, and for hours. Paulo Sérgio Neves de Souza on 10 July 2026, Belline Santana on 25 August, Daniel Vorcaro on 28 August, the last of them from prison. What follows is the essence of each version and, at the end, where they meet and where they do not.

## The deputy

Paulo Sérgio said that, suspended for seven months and without access to e-mails and computers, he had written "about 100 pages" from memory. His story is that of someone who says he was the first to see the fraud. In 2024, his team discovered transactions guaranteed by a "liquidity fund" which, in October, turned out to be full of "BESC paper" (*cártulas do BESC*). In September 2024, he said, director Ailton telephoned him saying that senators were complaining to Galípolo, then in his confirmation hearing, about persecution of Master, and concluded: "as soon as Galípolo becomes president, we'll sort out the problem". Paulo Sérgio calls this a request to "hold off" (*jogar parado*), and says he reacted by having the finding formalised in a letter of 15 September 2024.

He recounted the meeting with BTG on 3 December 2024, the "R$ 32 billion of fraud" which, once Will and the adjustments were deducted, came to something close to the R$ 11.5 billion the supervision already had; that he always defended the BTG solution, passed over in favour of BRB; that he prepared a liquidation vote on 25 March 2025 and that the team produced "23 versions of liquidation votes" up to November. On the systemic risk Ailton attributes to him: "systemic risk was never mentioned, but rather the lowest cost to society". On the second fraud, the one with the portfolios, he recounted the scene of 3 June 2025, when Ailton, after a meeting with BRB's president, told director Renato "we have a bonus", and he replied "Are you kidding? It's over!". His team asked Augusto Lima for a hundred contracts and "none existed"; the notification to the prosecution service went out on 15 July.

On the messages, his thesis is a single one: the Code of Conduct prohibits paid consultancy to third parties, not "the supervisor's work with the supervised", which "constantly consists of reviewing processes". The appointment order was sent because "from then on the deputy was the one responsible"; the "caught in the filter" was the daily monitoring set up in December 2024; the tips for the meeting with Campos Neto came about because Vorcaro "was desperate" about two new rules. On "angel": "it was that angel who discovered the fraud in September 2024".

On Disney: he told Vorcaro he would be taking the family; Vorcaro said "the bank has a package"; he accepted "as long as it was paid for"; he took "the place of a cancellation", paid "approximately 8,000" on arrival and "doesn't even know who this Léo is". On the farm: he was asking R$ 2 million plus 10% of the lots, they offered R$ 1.8 million, he closed in January 2020, and the total, with a 2022 addendum because of the rise in coffee prices, reached R$ 4.8 million, paid "from the buyer's personal account" and from Pipe. He learned that Zettel was Vorcaro's brother-in-law on 13 January 2021, at the deed, when Zettel told him "I'm estranged from him, I have nothing to do with the bank". He did not notify the Central Bank: "what would the conflict of interest be". On the Guaxupé house: he built it, sold it to Noah on 30 January 2024 for a little over R$ 1 million in 28 instalments, almost all of them already paid, with a buy-back clause for when the lots came through, the gym provided for in the contract and no deed "so as not to pay fee 1 and fee 2". Asked whether the two deals, both without the buyer taking possession, were not strange, he answered that "the farm is his, the company is his and the deals are his". And he turned the strangeness around: if the sale was a bribe, "then Vorcaro pays him a bribe in 2020", and he "was one of the first to leave" the directorate.

On 17 November, he "honestly has no such recollection" of having pressed Ailton; he learned of the buyer, Fictor, at 5 pm, from the press, "because the guy sponsored Palmeiras".

## The head

Belline opened with his career: an official since 1998, head of Desup since October 2019, vice-president of the association of supervisors of the Americas in 2024, IMF consultant. And with his biography: Black, from the Jequitinhonha Valley, asked "whether he was his own team's driver" when already head of department. The racial question, he said, "has always been a matter of great personal importance".

His version of the contract: in August 2023, on a trip to Panama, he received a call from Leonardo Palhares, who introduced himself as chairman of the Chamber and representative of an international law firm, and said he had obtained his name "through various sources", among them Vorcaro. He asked Vorcaro whether he had recommended him; Vorcaro confirmed. He consulted "informally" the then head of internal affairs and the secretary of the ethics committee, of which he was a member, about taking part in an "educational project"; he was told there was no problem and no need to formalise it. He admitted that, in those consultations, he mentioned neither the amount nor Vorcaro's recommendation: the object of the consultation "was the lawfulness of the relationship, and not the amount". He negotiated "directly with Leonardo Palhares", who proposed the R$ 2 million; he "never had any kind of contact" with Zettel. First contract in September 2023, four instalments of R$ 500,000 from October to January; second contract in September 2024, two instalments of R$ 500,000 in October and November and, from March 2025, irregular instalments of R$ 100,000, first to him personally, then to Inspiração, set up in June 2025; total for the second, R$ 1.815 million. Sum: R$ 3.815 million. On the proportion, "he has no benchmark". He delivered "twenty-two deliverables", a report, podcasts, a brand, Instagram. He learned that Palhares was a director of Super only in the internal inquiry, and asked to be released.

On the messages, his key is the board's strategy: "from 4 December 2024" the director received a daily liquidity report, the Central Bank "was seeking the lowest-cost solution", and "we need to get to the other side" was that, not cheering. The WhatsApp group was created "in pursuit of efficiency". The meetings at the house in Brasília, two, and in São Paulo, about three, were "institutional". On Paris: he travelled with his wife to a wedding in Portugal, with a stopover organised by an agency; Vorcaro "did not even know he was in Paris" until he called, on the night of 10 September, about the sale of a stake; he offered a restaurant; he declined, received a reservation message, said thank you, "was very tired" and stayed at the hotel. "He has never been to those restaurants." On the approval of Augusto Lima as controller of Banco Pleno, ten days after the fraud notification, he said he was only asked whether there was a "legal impediment", and there was none. He read the police chief paragraph 329 of the report of the federal audit court (TCU), which speaks of the supervision's "diligence, timeliness and professional scepticism". "Never", he answered, when asked whether he had favoured anyone or passed on information in advance.

## The banker

Vorcaro thanked the police chief for hearing him, "nine months trying to speak". To the opening question, whether he paid or gave anything to the two officials, he answered "directly, no", and "through someone, no", but "he is aware of the transactions". His version of the project: at a meeting, Belline said he could not attend another one because he would be doing work with an association of Black people in central banks; the conversation turned to social initiatives; he "asked his brother-in-law" to approach Belline and "gave a range of figures": "two million and then to move up to four", "more or less what he put into each of the social projects". Belline, he said, did not know that he was the sponsor. The funding came from "the company Super, which belonged to his brother-in-law", and Super "at that moment was not linked" to him; Palhares's description of it as an asset-protection structure of his "is not 100% correct". "What a circus": "he does not specifically recall", but "he certainly would not call a social project a circus". The lists with "Bellini": project nicknames. And, "to be quite honest", he admitted that "because he was from the Central Bank" he took "greater care so that the facts did not appear to be something different from what they were". Ana Cláudia "is being wronged", "an operational person".

On the farm: a lawyer of his brought him the deal in 2019 and he passed it on to his brother-in-law; Pipe, "he did not even know existed". On Disney: he had "a marketing structure" with a concierge, sent "dozens of people", and the guide was "a treat" within a budget that in the last year had reached "almost five hundred million"; whether Paulo Sérgio paid, "he does not recall, believing not". Reviewing drafts before sending them "is a more than normal practice". On "angel": angels were "people not under the influence of the banking cartel, which wanted Banco Máxima liquidated". On the notes with the names of police chiefs: "there were people who kept him informed", "he does not recall who"; who the friends inside the bank were, "he does not remember". On 17 November: he told the Central Bank in the morning that he had a solution, with investors from the Emirates, and "realised when he was behind bars". On the frauds: "there were no frauds, there were problems that were solved".

## Where the versions cross

On Belline's money, the three versions agree on one point and differ on another. They agree that it came from companies linked to Zettel and Vorcaro and passed through Varajo. They differ on who knew: Palhares says the figure came from above; Vorcaro says he set the figure and that Belline did not know; Belline says he negotiated with Palhares and never spoke to Zettel.

On the total, the numbers do not add up. Belline said R$ 2 million in January, and R$ 3.815 million in August; Ailton heard "four million" in the internal inquiry; Palhares speaks of R$ 2.7 to 2.8 million; the spreadsheets on the phone add up to more. On the farm: R$ 4.5 million in January, R$ 4.8 million in July, R$ 3 million in the deed. On 17 November: Ailton says the two insisted; Paulo Sérgio does not remember; Vorcaro says he told the bank in the morning.

What none of the versions disputes is the set of messages. What all of them dispute is its meaning. Paulo Sérgio says it is supervision; Belline says it is the board's strategy; Vorcaro says it is the normal relationship of a bank under scrutiny. The police say it is paid consultancy. The Supreme Court, up to September 2026, has said nothing beyond "extended by sixty days".

> Reading rule number thirty-three: three people, three versions, and all of them fit the same messages. That is what a corruption inquiry without a confession looks like: the facts are settled, the meaning is not. What will decide it is not who speaks best. It is the bank statement that has not yet arrived.
