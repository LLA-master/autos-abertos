Every assumption of jurisdiction carries a temptation: to grow. If the Supreme Court has jurisdiction over "all the subject matter, including related matter", the next question is what counts as related, and who decides. In December 2025 that question was asked in São Paulo, answered by a substitute federal judge, and the answer was no. This chapter is about that no, about the opinion of the Prosecutor General's Office and about the decision of 22 December that closed the complaint in less than a month.

## Quasar

After the interim order of 9 December, Vorcaro's defence went to the 7th Federal Criminal Court of São Paulo, where Operation Quasar is pending, launched on 28 August 2025 against fund administrators, securities distributors and a payment institution. The defence asked the court to check whether there were "investigative proceedings or measures related" to Banco Master or to the petitioner and to inform the Supreme Court, "so as to safeguard the authority of the Supreme Court's decision". The press had been linking Master to operations Carbono Oculto, Quasar and Tank; the petition itself, according to the Federal Police, acknowledged that "such a link is entirely improper", but asked for the check all the same.

The Federal Police in São Paulo replied that Vorcaro "is not among the suspects", that "no connection whatsoever can be seen" with the sale of portfolios to BRB, and that Toffoli's interim order "did not establish a universal jurisdiction over each and every act carried out within the said financial institution, but only ordered the joinder of investigations related to Operation Compliance Zero". The news reports, it said, were due to the fact that REAG, one of the distributors under investigation, "is responsible for managing part of Banco Master's funds". The federal prosecution service (MPF), through its organised-crime unit, took the same line: Quasar "does not have Banco Master as its main, or even secondary, target", and the claim of a connection, "being based solely on media reports", "finds no support in the record".

Judge Paulo Cezar Duran refused the transfer on 19 December. But he placed on record something the defence may not have wished to see recorded. A police intelligence report, produced "from the material extracted from the mobile phone" of one of the Quasar suspects, "makes references to BANCO MASTER S.A. and to DANIEL BUENO VORCARO". The decision transcribes the passage: a person, whose name is omitted, "acts as the link between DANIEL VORCARO (DV), the ultimate beneficiary of the operations, and [the distributor's director], technically responsible for executing the demands in the funds administered by TRUSTEE"; the conversations "reveal practices that indicate accounting manipulation, corporate restructurings on demand, the use of investment funds and SPVs to acquire assets and adjust valuations", with "ironic remarks that evoke glamorised illicit practices". That "fortuitous discovery of evidence" had been shared, on 24 September 2025, "for the benefit of a police inquiry pending before another Court", which "will have to reassess its own jurisdiction". The judge sent a copy of everything to Toffoli. In short: Quasar is not about Master, but inside it there was a phone that talked about Master, and what that phone said had been, since September, in another inquiry that this archive does not contain.

## The opinion

The Prosecutor General's Office, which had not yet been heard when the interim order came out, gave its view before the final decision, and the opinion, transcribed in the decision, is a model of agreement with reservations. "The element justifying the precautionary measure is the seizure of a purchase option agreement for a property involving Federal Congressman João Carlos Bacelar. Without that element, there would be no reason for the proceeding not to continue running in the trial court." The precautionary measure "is adequate", "at least until, if it comes to that, the investigation's bearing on a holder of privileged venue is ruled out".

But "it must be emphasised that the Congressman's name only surfaced after the measure ordered by the trial court had been carried out. Therefore, the search and seizure order itself and the evidence obtained up to that point obviously remain intact and sound. The discovery was accidental." And on the detention, which "no longer subsists, which empties the request of its object", the PGR made a point of saying that "the validity of its imposition before the decision in this case should be recognised as regards the point of jurisdiction". The conclusion: "partial admission of the complaint", "preservation of the soundness of the acts issued in the trial court", continuation of the inquiry under Toffoli, "without prejudice to the question of original jurisdiction being reviewed later". In other words, the prosecution accepted losing the Brasília judge, but did not accept that what he had done was void.

## The decision

The final decision runs to 17 pages and is dated 22 December 2025, a month less five days after the initial petition. It reproduces at length two precedents: the point of order in Criminal Action 937, of 2018, which restricted privileged venue to "crimes committed during the exercise of the office and related to the functions performed", and Habeas Corpus 232.627, of 2025, which decided that the prerogative "subsists even after the person leaves office". Then comes the sentence that defines the complaint: "among the individuals mentioned in the investigation who, in theory, could come to be subject to the jurisdiction of this Supreme Court by reason of holding offices with privileged venue, only in relation to one of them, Federal Congressman J.C.B., are there, in fact, indicative elements of a possible criminal offence."

Read it carefully. "Individuals mentioned", in the plural, "who could come to be subject": there are other names with privileged venue in the record, and about them the decision does not say there are indications. About the congressman, it says there are "indicative elements", but "at this initial stage, it is not yet possible to state whether the alleged conducts, which may eventually be attributed to him, bear a direct relation to the exercise of the parliamentary mandate". That is: it is not known whether the crime, if there is one, belongs to the office. And it is precisely because it is not known that the Supreme Court declares itself competent: "even in such cases, it falls to the Supreme Federal Court to exercise judicial supervision of the investigation, in order to ensure the regularity of the evidentiary acts and, in due course, to define, with greater precision, the presence, or not, of a link". Uncertainty is the ground of jurisdiction, not its obstacle.

The decision repeats Marco Aurélio's 2014 opinion: "from the moment indications emerge, mere indications, of the participation of a holder of privileged venue in the facts, the judicial authority must decline jurisdiction, and not persist in performing acts aimed at deepening the investigation". And it closes with the refusal to sever: "at this still embryonic stage of the investigations, given the apparent intertwining of the conducts and the interdependence of the actions narrated, it is not convenient to sever the investigations relating to the agents, natural or legal persons, in the factual context under investigation".

The operative part: complaint "partially upheld, to recognise the jurisdiction of this Supreme Court to supervise the investigations involving operation 'Compliance Zero'". Kept, "all the administrative, civil and criminal decisions already issued in relation to all the suspects, including the restraints on goods and funds". Kept, "the alternative measures set by the Federal Court of the 1st Region", because "there is, in principle, no urgency in the matter, nor any way to verify a change in the factual situation before receipt of all the documents I have requested, which has not happened so far". "Once final, to the archive."

## What the decision did not do

It is worth listing. It did not annul any act of the Brasília judge, which is what the PGR had asked for and the defence had feared. It did not revoke or loosen the alternative measures, which Vorcaro and Alberto Felix asked for three times; the ankle monitor of a treasury superintendent dismissed for cause stayed where it was. It did not decide on the congressional inquiry, on BRB's properties, on the search at Banco Pleno. It did not sever. And it did not say, anywhere, what was in the folder beyond a "purchase option agreement for a property". The congressman, who asked for and obtained access to the file on 9 December, did not file again in the documents this site has read.

What the decision did was transfer an investigation of two banks, five former detainees and sixteen billion reais in freezes to the supervision of one justice, on the strength of a document about a property found in a folder, whose relation to the mandate of the folder's owner the decision itself declares it does not know. This is not a criticism; it is the Court's case law rigorously applied, as the decision demonstrates in seventeen pages of citations. It is also, for anyone reading from outside, a demonstration of how little is enough.

On 23 December, the reporting justice ordered COAF, the financial-intelligence unit, to observe the interim order. On 9 February 2026, he ordered COAF to send its reports to Inquiry 5.026, "pending before this Supreme Court". On 10 February, he certified that the decision had become final and ordered the case archived, with the proviso that "pending incidental questions may be assessed within INQ 5.026". Three days later, the inquiry would be redistributed to another justice. The complaint had done its job.

> Reading rule number nineteen: "mere indications" is the most powerful expression in Brazilian criminal procedure. It moves a case from one building to another without anyone having to prove anything, and the decision that uses it usually says, honestly, that it does not yet know.
