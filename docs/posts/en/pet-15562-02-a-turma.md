The word is the police's, but it came from the messages. "A Turma" (the crew), with a capital letter and in quotation marks, is how Felipe Mourão refers, in his conversations with Daniel Vorcaro, to the group that carries out what the banker asks. The request calls it the "armed wing" and draws it in a diagram, on page 60: at the top, Vorcaro; below him, Mourão, who coordinates; below him, the "Turma", "made up of police officers, including one federal police officer", and "the boys" (*os meninos*), a group "not yet fully identified" that handles the digital side. This chapter is about how the police say they arrived at the name of the only member they identified, and about what they say the Turma did and how much it cost.

## The drone

The identification begins with a domestic episode. In April 2024, according to the report of 26 February, Vorcaro "instructed the 'Sicário' to identify the person responsible for a drone that was circling his residence in Belo Horizonte". Mourão replied that he had "'A turma' on the phone" and forwarded a voice note from someone who asked for the names of the people who worked at the property, "so that a background check could be run on them", and warned that they had not found the drone, "probably because of the rain". On 6 April 2024, at 11:22 am, Mourão sent a photo of the entrance to a gated community in Nova Lima and another voice note in the same voice: the man said he "was in front of Gate B" and "needed clearance to get in".

That is how the police say they pinned down the name. They wrote to the community's management, which replied with the list of authorised visitors: Vorcaro had cleared the entry of Marilson Roseno da Silva that day, at 11:03 am, "exactly as the federal police officer himself recounted in the voice note". Cross-checked against the staff rolls of the Federal Police's regional headquarters in Minas Gerais, that led to an officer retired in August 2022. The request calls him "leader of the so-called 'Turma'" and "a relevant component of the armed wing". The justice, more restrained, describes him as someone who "used his professional experience and the contacts acquired in his police career to help obtain sensitive data", "under the leadership" of Mourão.

In the messages he always appears second-hand: his voice notes forwarded by Mourão. On 15 February 2024, Vorcaro asked whether "they know anything about this IPL", giving a case number from the Federal Court in São Paulo; forty minutes later, Mourão sent a voice note from Marilson saying "that he had already made contact with the 'Tropa' and that they would give the request special attention". On 15 September 2025, another voice note of his asks whether Mourão "had forwarded 'the partial results of the checks'" to Vorcaro and presses for "the monthly payment to the group 'A turma'". Right afterwards, a screenshot "of an internal Federal Police system showing police inquiry numbers". The police recognise the screen: "it is a screen of the ePol system", with the result of a search for "Daniel Vorcaro".

## The name whited out

Before the Federal Police, the target was the systems of the prosecution service. On 23 July 2024, Mourão sent "a new search carried out in the MPF's systems", with Vorcaro's taxpayer number as the parameter: "15 proceedings related to him, of which 11 were sealed". The user responsible only appeared from the second page of the PDF onwards. On analysing the file, the police say they noticed that someone "had tried to hide their identification by applying a white hatching over the field containing their name and department". The data was still there, "and could be recovered simply by selecting, copying and pasting the covered section". It was the login of an MPF employee, whose name is in the record and not here: the report of 19 February itself notes that the use may have been "with or without conscious participation".

In the same sequence, a search with the parameter "BRB AND MASTER". Vorcaro replies: "I want everything. Full attention". Mourão: "Yes! So I'll have them pull everything. Ok". The next day, 24 July, Mourão forwarded a preliminary case file (*notícia de fato*) from the federal prosecutors' office in the Federal District whose summary read "crime against the national financial system. To investigate the possible occurrence of crimes against the national financial system and related crimes in the acquisition of Banco Master S/A by Banco de Brasília". The request underlines: "the aforementioned preliminary case file was the starting point of Operation Compliance Zero". In other words, according to the police, the suspect had in his hands, sixteen months before his arrest, the document that opened the investigation against him.

There is a subtlety of dates that the filing does not resolve. The search is dated 23 July 2024, but the next sentence says that "at least from 23/07/2025, Daniel Bueno Vorcaro began to map any investigation". One of the two years is wrong, and the text does not say which.

## The Interpol that was not

On 24 October 2025, at 4:31 pm, Mourão forwarded "a screenshot with the Interpol logo" and messages: "What is in white was to hide the name of whoever ran the search", "We are waiting for the main FBI report", "Interpol is clean". The name searched was Vorcaro's. The police consulted Interpol's office in Brazil, which replied that "the layout shared does not correspond to search mechanisms made available by Interpol", but that "it may be a search mechanism of another country" with an offline database. The request's conclusion is cautious: "it is likely that the search was carried out through a national system integrated with an 'offline' database", as the United States do, for example, and identifying the user "would only be possible for the country that maintains that system". The justice, in the ruling, wrote "international ones such as Interpol" without that caveat.

A year earlier, on 11 October 2024, Mourão had sent Vorcaro "a document entitled 'Interpol'", and Vorcaro told him "not to send the document before they aligned". The letter carried the letterhead of the federal prosecution service and cited a Federal Police inquiry in Roraima which, the police verified, had been opened "to investigate the use of a forged document, that is, an entirely different subject". The request concludes that the document "was forged by the Turma" and that "it remains to be clarified whether or not they used the said document to actually request support from Interpol".

## The boys

The other half of the diagram handles profiles and news. On 24 April 2024, Vorcaro sent an Instagram link and wrote: "take it down and go after him. Take everything". Mourão: "ok. Now"; then, that "the boys are on it" and that the account was verified, "which is why it was taking longer to come down". In October 2024, the target was a businessman who ran an investor community and had criticised the banker. Mourão: "already had the WhatsApp taken down". Vorcaro: "Have to wait. I'll give notice. The guy has a market community with thousands of people". The next day: "Lives in Morumbi they went there. What do you want done?". Then: "The boys are inside the guy's iCloud". The banker's reply: "good". The police note that the same businessman filed a police report and told the press, in December 2025, that he had spent around R$ 50,000 to recover his numbers.

The method for taking down profiles is what most impresses the police: "irregular use of Law Enforcement Information Request portals", the channels the platforms reserve for the authorities, "through institutional users". In October 2024, a fake profile bearing the name of Vorcaro's girlfriend; he writes "I need this profile taken down" and "This son of a bitch I want found even if it's in hell". Two days later, the "turma" sends him a copy of the letter sent to Meta: the letterhead of an anti-corruption group of the Ceará state prosecution service, "with no valid digital signature" and with the prosecutor's name spelt one way in the header and another in the signature. The e-mail used, that one, was genuine and official, belonging to an employee of that prosecution service, about whom the police say they cannot "determine whether there was conscious participation". "On the same day", the filing records, "the target account was banned".

## The bill

What all of this cost, the police deduce from spreadsheets and payment orders. On 15 February 2024, at 7:01 pm, Vorcaro "told Fabiano Zettel to include" Mourão "on the monthly payment list to receive R$ 1,000,000.00". On 13 March, he chases the month's payment; Zettel says he "would do the TED transfer letter". On 19 July, Zettel says he "needs to make the transfer to King", and Vorcaro: "When I ask don't let it fail. It was important". On 16 October, another reminder, and Zettel: "Every month I send it". On 11 November 2024, Zettel sends a list of who was chasing him; on it, "Sicário", R$ 1 million, and a reporter, who, according to the police, was being paid R$ 2 million. In the police reading of the messages, Vorcaro authorises both payments.

In 2025 the hand changes. On 14 July, Ana Cláudia asks Vorcaro whether it is "to include R$ 1,000,000.00 on the list as usual"; he says yes; she sends five receipts, one of them for a Pix transfer of R$ 1 million to King, Mourão's company. On 10 November 2025, eight days before the liquidation, she sends the spreadsheet "Accounts payable, Super, November 2025", and Vorcaro authorises the million for King. In one of the spreadsheets shared by Zettel, "Sicário" sits on the line of monthly expenses of R$ 6.55 million.

The police's arithmetic: payments since October 2023, "as a rule R$ 1,000,000.00 a month", plus bonuses, so they "exceeded the figure of R$ 24,000,000.00". And, "according to the accounts presented by Sicário", the Turma "received R$ 400,000.00 a month, to be divided among 6 members", which would come to "up to R$ 9,600,000.00". The report of 26 February is more prudent than the request: it says Mourão "may have received, at a minimum" R$ 24 million and that "the identification of the other members" and "the individualisation of their conduct require the investigations to continue". The justice stayed in the middle: "significant monthly remuneration", "subsequently distributed among members of the informal structure".

Of the six, one has a name. Of the other five, the police had, in March, a voice note, a screenshot and a collective nickname.

> Reading rule number thirty-five: a group with a name, a nickname, a salary and a boss, but only one identified member, is halfway between an organisation and a hypothesis. What separates the two is not the police chief's conviction. It is the visitor log of a gatehouse, which exists, and the payroll of the other five, which does not yet.
